背景

罕病診斷的核心難題：VUS

臨床基因檢測常見的瓶頸不是「找不到變異」，而是 VUS（Variant of Uncertain Significance）——找到了，但不知道是不是致病。ClinVar 大約 50% 提交為 VUS。AI 變異效應預測器 (VEP) 的工作就是把「不確定」往「可能致病 / 可能良性」推。

2023 年 Science 封面：AlphaMissense 涵蓋人類 7100 萬個錯義變異，AUROC ≈ 0.96，覆蓋率前所未見。同年 Illumina 推 PrimateAI-3D，用非人靈長類變異訓練。2024 起 Meta ESM1b / ESM-3 用語言模型 zero-shot 給分。

The bottleneck in clinical genetic testing isn't "no variant found" — it's VUS (Variant of Uncertain Significance): variant found but pathogenicity unclear. ~50% of ClinVar submissions are VUS. AI variant effect predictors (VEPs) push "uncertain" toward "likely pathogenic / likely benign".

Science cover 2023: AlphaMissense covers all 71M human missense variants at AUROC ≈ 0.96. The same year, Illumina launched PrimateAI-3D, trained on non-human primate variants. Since 2024, Meta's ESM1b / ESM-3 provides zero-shot scores via language modeling.

ACMG 整合

AI 分數與 ACMG 規範

ACMG/AMP 2015 框架把致病性分成 P/LP/VUS/LB/B，每條證據有 PVS / PS / PM / PP（致病）與 BA / BS / BP（良性）等強度。AI 分數通常落在 PP3（電腦預測支持致病）/ BP4（電腦預測支持良性）。2023 修訂的 SVI (Sequence Variant Interpretation) workgroup 文件給了 quantitative thresholds：

  • AlphaMissense ≥ 0.564 ⇒ PP3_Strong；≤ 0.34 ⇒ BP4_Strong（依 cohort 校準）。
  • AlphaMissense ≥ 0.564 ⇒ PP3_Strong; ≤ 0.34 ⇒ BP4_Strong (cohort-calibrated).
  • REVEL ≥ 0.932 ⇒ PP3_Very Strong；≤ 0.016 ⇒ BP4_Very Strong。
  • REVEL ≥ 0.932 ⇒ PP3_Very Strong; ≤ 0.016 ⇒ BP4_Very Strong.
  • SpliceAI ≥ 0.5 ⇒ PP3 for splice region；< 0.1 ⇒ BP4。
  • SpliceAI ≥ 0.5 ⇒ PP3 splice; < 0.1 ⇒ BP4.

想再深一點？AlphaMissense 的 fine-tuning

AlphaMissense 不是從頭訓練，而是把 AlphaFold 2 當成「蛋白質結構的先驗」做 fine-tuning。輸入：(WT seq, variant seq, MSA, structure)。輸出：致病性機率 [0, 1]。

關鍵設計：

  • Label noise robust—訓練標籤不是「ClinVar 真實致病性」，而是 frequency-derived weak label：在人口/靈長類中常見 → 推測 benign；從未觀察 → 推測 likely pathogenic。
  • Structure-aware—loss 函數中包含對 AlphaFold structure prediction 的一致性項；變異破壞結構越多，pathogenicity score 越高。
  • Calibration via observed frequency—最終分數用 gnomAD 觀察頻率做校準。

為何 PrimateAI-3D 在罕病 cohort 反超？因為 AlphaMissense 訓練用了 human variants 做 benign 來源；PrimateAI-3D 用純非人靈長類變異，避免「人類觀察=benign」的 circular reasoning。在真正罕病 cohort 上，benign vs pathogenic 的邊界更清楚。

AlphaMissense doesn't train from scratch — it fine-tunes AlphaFold 2 as a structural prior. Input: (WT seq, variant seq, MSA, structure). Output: pathogenicity probability [0, 1].

Key designs:

  • Label-noise robust — labels aren't "ClinVar truth" but frequency-derived weak labels: common in humans/primates → benign; never observed → likely pathogenic.
  • Structure-aware — loss includes consistency with AlphaFold structure prediction; the more disruptive the variant, the higher its pathogenicity score.
  • Calibration via observed frequency — final score calibrated by gnomAD allele frequency.

Why does PrimateAI-3D win on rare-disease cohorts? AlphaMissense used human variants for the benign training set; PrimateAI-3D uses pure non-human primate variants, avoiding circular reasoning "humans observed = benign". On true rare-disease cohorts, the benign/pathogenic boundary becomes clearer.

案例故事
2024 · 一個 VUS 翻盤的故事

一個 VUS 翻盤救了一個家庭

2024 年波士頓兒童醫院遺傳科：一個 7 歲女孩因癲癇 + 發展遲緩做 WES，找到一個 SCN8A 基因 c.4949G>A (p.R1650Q) 變異。傳統工具評估：

  • ClinVar：VUS（沒人報過）
  • REVEL：0.74（中間值）
  • CADD PHRED：23.5（borderline）
  • SIFT / PolyPhen：分歧

遺傳諮詢師原本要回報「VUS，無法給臨床建議」。但團隊接著查了 AlphaMissense=0.92, PrimateAI-3D=0.89, ESM1b=−7.8 (strongly disruptive)。三個 AI 一致指向 likely pathogenic。

結合 (1) de novo（trio WES 證實父母都沒有此變異）+ (2) ACMG PM2 (gnomAD 頻率=0) + (3) PP3_Strong (3 個 AI 高分一致) + (4) PS2 (de novo with phenotype consistency)，最終評為 Likely Pathogenic。

女孩接著被診斷為 SCN8A-related epileptic encephalopathy，導入鈉通道阻斷劑（如 carbamazepine）後癲癇控制顯著改善——這正是這類患者的標準治療。沒有 AI VEP 整合，她可能會被持續使用 levetiracetam 等「無效或反效果」的藥物。

2024, Boston Children's Hospital: a 7-year-old girl with epilepsy + developmental delay had WES finding a SCN8A c.4949G>A (p.R1650Q) variant. Classical tools:

  • ClinVar: VUS (never reported)
  • REVEL: 0.74 (middle)
  • CADD PHRED: 23.5 (borderline)
  • SIFT / PolyPhen: disagreed

The counselor was about to report "VUS, no clinical guidance possible." Then the team checked AlphaMissense=0.92, PrimateAI-3D=0.89, ESM1b=−7.8 (strongly disruptive). All three AI scores aligned on likely pathogenic.

Combining (1) de novo (trio WES confirmed neither parent has it) + (2) ACMG PM2 (gnomAD frequency=0) + (3) PP3_Strong (3 AI scores consistent high) + (4) PS2 (de novo with phenotype fit), the variant was reclassified as Likely Pathogenic.

The girl was diagnosed with SCN8A-related epileptic encephalopathy; switching to sodium-channel blockers (e.g., carbamazepine) dramatically improved seizure control — the standard treatment for this condition. Without AI VEP integration, she might have stayed on ineffective or counter-effective drugs like levetiracetam.

教訓：AI VEP 不是「取代 ACMG」，而是「強化 PP3/BP4 證據」；3 個分數一致比單一分數更可靠。
Lesson: AI VEPs don't replace ACMG — they strengthen PP3/BP4 evidence; 3-score consensus beats any single score.

想得更深

Q1. AI VEP 真的可以取代 functional assay 嗎？AlphaMissense AUROC 0.96 看似驚人，但 MAVE 直接量測仍是黃金標準。SVI 2023 仍把「functional study (PS3)」置於最高層級。辯論：對「結構性」變異 AI 可靠；對「調控性」變異仍需濕實驗。
Q1. Can AI VEPs really replace functional assays?AlphaMissense AUROC 0.96 looks impressive, but MAVE direct measurement remains the gold standard. SVI 2023 still places "functional study (PS3)" at the highest tier.Debate: AI reliable for structural variants; regulatory variants still need wet labs.
Q2. 跨族裔的 PRS 為什麼會失準？UKB-trained PRS 在東亞 R² 下降 50–70%（Martin 2019 Nat Genet）。原因：LD 結構差異 + 等位基因頻率差異 + 環境互動差異。解法：本地族群 GWAS（TWB、BBJ）+ trans-ancestry PRS 方法（PRS-CSx）。
Q2. Why do cross-ancestry PRS fail?UKB-trained PRS drops R² 50–70% on East Asians (Martin 2019 Nat Genet). Reasons: LD differences + allele frequencies + environmental interactions.Fix: local-ancestry GWAS (TWB, BBJ) + trans-ancestry PRS methods (PRS-CSx).
Q3. ClinVar AUROC 與罕病 cohort AUROC 差距代表什麼？0.96 → 0.78 的差距不是 model failure，是 evaluation distribution shift——ClinVar 的「benign」常是 well-studied、low-impact 變異，與罕病 cohort 真實 differentials 不同。行動：申請罕病臨床 cohort 做本地 benchmark。
Q3. What does the ClinVar AUROC vs rare-disease AUROC gap mean?0.96 → 0.78 isn't model failure — it's evaluation distribution shift. ClinVar's "benigns" tend to be well-studied, low-impact variants, unlike rare-disease cohort true differentials.Action: build local rare-disease cohort for benchmarking.
Q4. 非編碼變異（non-coding）AI 預測為何困難？Enformer、Borzoi 在「TSS 附近」尚可，距離 100kb+ 表現大幅下降；MAVE-style noncoding screens 仍稀少。前沿：cMP-MPRA、STARR-seq 是當前濕實驗 ground truth。
Q4. Why is non-coding variant prediction hard?Enformer, Borzoi okay near TSS but degrade at 100kb+; MAVE-style non-coding screens still scarce.Frontier: cMP-MPRA, STARR-seq are current wet-lab ground truth.
