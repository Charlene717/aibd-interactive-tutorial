多體學整合與空間組

RNA + ATAC + 蛋白 + 空間位置——一個細胞的「全相」要怎麼用一個模型同時看？

RNA + ATAC + protein + spatial coordinates — how to view a cell's full picture through one model?

新手友善 中階 進階補充

這一章你會學到什麼？

不管你只跑過 RNA-seq、剛接觸 CITE-seq，還是想做空間多體學——這一章是你的多模態地圖。讀完你會：

  • 說出 MOFA+、totalVI、MultiVI、SpatialGlue、Seurat WNN 五大整合方法的差異
  • 分辨「水平整合」（同樣本多模態）vs「垂直整合」（多樣本同模態）的策略
  • 知道 CITE-seq、Visium、Xenium、MERFISH 等技術何時用 / 不用
  • 避開「越多模態越好」「unpaired 多體學一定行」的迷思

Whether you've only done RNA-seq, just touched CITE-seq, or want spatial multi-omics — this is your multimodal map:

  • Differentiate MOFA+, totalVI, MultiVI, SpatialGlue, Seurat WNN
  • Distinguish horizontal (same sample, multi-modality) vs vertical (multi-sample, same modality) integration
  • Know when to use / avoid CITE-seq, Visium, Xenium, MERFISH
  • Steer clear of "more modalities is always better" and "unpaired multi-omics works" myths
為何整合

一個細胞有多重身分

RNA 表現只告訴你「正在轉錄什麼」；ATAC 告訴你「染色質開放在哪裡」；CITE-seq 告訴你「細胞表面有什麼蛋白」；空間轉錄組告訴你「細胞跟誰當鄰居」。多體學整合的目標不是把訊號堆疊，而是學一個共同的潛在空間，讓不同模態互相校正、互相補位。

2024 起，scverse 生態（scvi-tools / Squidpy / SpatialData）讓多模態工作流終於有一致的資料容器與 API。

RNA tells you "what's being transcribed"; ATAC tells you "which chromatin is open"; CITE-seq tells you "surface proteins"; spatial transcriptomics tells you "who lives next to whom." The goal of multi-omics integration is not stacking signals but learning a shared latent space where modalities calibrate and complement each other.

Since 2024, the scverse ecosystem (scvi-tools / Squidpy / SpatialData) finally offers consistent containers and APIs for multi-modal workflows.

adata = sc.read_h5ad("citeseq.h5ad")

    protein_expression_obsm_key="protein_expression",
    batch_key="batch")
m = scvi.model.TOTALVI(adata, latent_distribution="normal")

adata.obsm["denoised_protein"] = m.get_normalized_expression()[1]

想再深一點？totalVI 的 VAE + 蛋白前景比

CITE-seq 把 RNA-seq 加上 antibody-derived tag (ADT)，每細胞同時測 mRNA 與表面蛋白。問題：ADT 有顯著背景訊號（同型抗體未結合的雜訊）。

totalVI 用 two-component mixture：每個 protein 在每個 cell 的真實表達量 ρ 是 foreground rate + background rate 加權混合：

y_p = π_p · NB(α_back) + (1−π_p) · NB(α_fore)

其中 π_p 由 latent z 解碼。這讓 totalVI 在 denoise 表面蛋白訊號時，自然分離真實 vs 背景，遠勝過單純 log-normalize。

RNA 端走 standard scVI 的 ZINB（zero-inflated negative binomial）模型。兩個 decoder 共享 latent z，這就是「跨模態共同表徵」的數學實現。

CITE-seq adds antibody-derived tags (ADT) to RNA-seq, measuring mRNA + surface protein per cell. Problem: ADT has substantial background (unbound isotype noise).

totalVI uses a two-component mixture: each protein per cell is a weighted blend of foreground and background rates:

y_p = π_p · NB(α_back) + (1−π_p) · NB(α_fore)

where π_p decodes from latent z. This naturally separates real signal from background, far surpassing simple log-normalization.

The RNA branch uses standard scVI's ZINB (zero-inflated negative binomial). Both decoders share a single latent z — that's the mathematical realization of "shared cross-modal representation".

案例故事
2024 · Visium HD + scRNA

Visium HD + scRNA 找腫瘤微環境新亞型

2024 一項對 CRC (大腸癌) 的研究：用 Visium HD（2 μm 解析度）做空間轉錄組，配上同樣本的 scRNA-seq。團隊用 SpatialGlue + Tangram 把兩者投到同一 latent space。

結果：在腫瘤邊界發現一個之前 scRNA 看不到的「neutrophil–fibroblast 互動帶」，這個亞型在 ICI 反應差的病人尤其明顯。沒有空間訊息，這個 niche 完全混在「中性球」cluster 內，導致 marker gene panel 無法區分。

啟示：空間 × 單細胞整合不是錦上添花，而是解鎖過去藏在「平均」中的細胞通訊邏輯。但要做好，pipeline 必須包含：(1) 空間 cell segmentation；(2) deconvolution；(3) cross-modal anchoring（如 Tangram、cell2location）；(4) 跨樣本對齊。

A 2024 CRC study used Visium HD (2 μm resolution) for spatial transcriptomics paired with scRNA-seq on the same samples. The team used SpatialGlue + Tangram to project both into a shared latent space.

Result: at the tumor border, they found a "neutrophil–fibroblast interaction zone" invisible in scRNA alone, especially prominent in ICI non-responders. Without spatial info, this niche blended into the neutrophil cluster — no marker panel could distinguish it.

Takeaway: spatial × single-cell isn't a nice-to-have — it unlocks cell-communication logic hidden in averages. But the pipeline must include: (1) spatial cell segmentation; (2) deconvolution; (3) cross-modal anchoring (Tangram, cell2location); (4) cross-sample alignment.

教訓：「越多模態越好」是錯的；「對的模態 + 對的整合算法」才是真實答案。
Lesson: "more modalities is better" is wrong; "the right modalities + the right integration algorithm" is the truth.

想得更深

Q1. 未配對 (unpaired) 多體學該如何整合？大多數現有方法假設「同細胞配對」；現實多是「不同樣本不同模態」。GLUE、Pamona、MMD-MA 等試圖無配對對齊，但效果仍弱於配對。參考：Cao 2022 Nat Biotech GLUE 的 graph-linked unified embedding。
Q1. How to integrate unpaired multi-omics?Most methods assume "paired cells"; reality often gives "different samples, different modalities". GLUE, Pamona, MMD-MA attempt unpaired alignment, weaker than paired.Reference: Cao 2022 Nat Biotech GLUE graph-linked unified embedding.
Q2. 空間轉錄組值得多花的成本嗎？Visium HD 約是 scRNA 的 3–5×，Xenium 8–10×。對腫瘤微環境、發育空間軸、neurodegeneration 是 yes；對 PBMC immune profiling 則 no。經驗：研究問題本身需要「空間鄰居」嗎？
Q2. Is spatial transcriptomics worth the extra cost?Visium HD costs 3–5× scRNA; Xenium 8–10×. Worth it for tumor TME, developmental axes, neurodegeneration; not for PBMC immune profiling.Test: does your question really need "spatial neighbors"?
Q3. CITE-seq vs 純 scRNA：研究設計取捨？多了 ~150 個表面蛋白訊息，但每細胞測序成本 ×2，且 ADT 標準化是另一個學習曲線。免疫細胞研究 yes；上皮細胞研究通常 no。參考：totalVI vs Seurat v5 WNN benchmark 顯示 totalVI 在標準化 ADT 上仍勝。
Q3. CITE-seq vs pure scRNA: study-design tradeoff?You gain ~150 surface proteins but cost doubles per cell, and ADT normalization is its own learning curve. Yes for immune; usually no for epithelial.Reference: totalVI vs Seurat v5 WNN benchmark — totalVI still wins on ADT normalization.
Q4. SpatialData 容器會取代 AnnData 嗎？scverse 2024 推出，野心很大；但很多現有工具還依賴 AnnData。短期 SpatialData 為「空間專用 wrapper」，AnnData 仍是核心。行動：寫新流程時直接用 SpatialData；既有 pipeline 不必急著遷移。
Q4. Will SpatialData replace AnnData?scverse's 2024 ambition is big, but many tools still depend on AnnData. Short-term SpatialData is a "spatial-specific wrapper"; AnnData remains core.Action: use SpatialData for new pipelines; don't rush to migrate existing ones.
常見問答
三條判準：(1) 單一模態解析不開的問題（如 CITE-seq 區分免疫亞型 / Visium 找空間 niche）；(2) 樣本量充足支援增加成本；(3) 有對應整合方法。如果你只想分 cluster，純 scRNA 已足夠。Three checks: (1) single-modality can't resolve the question (CITE-seq for immune subtypes / Visium for spatial niches); (2) sample size supports added cost; (3) integration method exists. Just for clustering? Pure scRNA suffices.
不是替代關係：MOFA+ 對「可解釋因子 + 跨樣本結構」最強，適合做 EWAS / phenotype 關聯；totalVI 對「同樣本多模態 denoising」最強，適合 CITE-seq downstream cluster + DE。實務上常 totalVI 先做 embedding，再用 MOFA+ 找跨樣本因子。Not competitors: MOFA+ excels at "interpretable factors + cross-sample structure" — good for EWAS / phenotype association; totalVI excels at "within-sample multi-modal denoising" — for CITE-seq clustering + DE. Practical combo: totalVI for embedding, then MOFA+ for cross-sample factors.
經驗：(1) 10x Visium—探索 3–6 樣本，發表 10–20；(2) Xenium / MERFISH—統計 power 高，5–10 樣本可有 80% power 偵測 1.5× FC。記得：每個 Visium slide 約 $1500-2500 USD，要嚴控預算。Rule of thumb: (1) 10x Visium — explore 3–6 samples, publish 10–20; (2) Xenium/MERFISH — high statistical power, 5–10 samples yield 80% power at 1.5× FC. Cost: each Visium slide ~$1,500–2,500 USD.
三大坑：(1) 不同細胞捕獲（ATAC 通常用核 vs RNA 用全細胞），導致 cell type 比例不一致；(2) 染色質可及性 ≠ 表達（時序差異）；(3) 稀疏度極端（ATAC 比 RNA 稀疏 ×10）。MultiVI / GLUE 都有專門設計處理。Three traps: (1) different cell capture (ATAC often nuclei, RNA whole cells), causing inconsistent cell-type proportions; (2) chromatin accessibility ≠ expression (temporal lag); (3) extreme sparsity (ATAC is ~10× sparser than RNA). MultiVI / GLUE have specific designs.
Visium HD 一張 slide 可達 1M spots（2 μm bin），SpatialGlue 在單 A100 訓練約 4–8 小時；推論 ~30 分鐘。記憶體吃緊建議 binning 到 8 μm 或 16 μm 起步，再做 multi-resolution 分析。A Visium HD slide can have 1M spots (2 μm bin); SpatialGlue trains in 4–8 hours on a single A100; inference ~30 min. Memory-tight? Start at 8 μm or 16 μm bins, then multi-resolution analysis.
  1. 多體學整合不是訊號相加，而是學共同 latent space，讓模態互相校正。
  2. 五大方法各有所長：MOFA+（可解釋因子）、totalVI（CITE-seq）、MultiVI（缺模態）、SpatialGlue（空間）、Seurat WNN（生態友善）。
  3. 水平 vs 垂直整合：同樣本多模 vs 多樣本同模，策略完全不同。
  4. 空間轉錄組第三代 (Xenium/MERFISH) 是「原位單分子」，徹底改寫腫瘤微環境研究。
  5. 未配對多體學仍是開放問題；現有方法（GLUE, Pamona）效果弱於配對。
  6. SpatialData 容器 是 2024 scverse 主推格式，但 AnnData 仍是核心。
  1. Multi-omics integration isn't signal addition — it's learning a shared latent space where modalities calibrate each other.
  2. Five methods, distinct strengths: MOFA+ (interpretable factors), totalVI (CITE-seq), MultiVI (missing modality), SpatialGlue (spatial), Seurat WNN (ecosystem).
  3. Horizontal vs vertical integration: same sample multi-mod vs multi-sample same-mod — completely different strategies.
  4. 3rd-gen spatial (Xenium/MERFISH) is "in-situ single-molecule" — rewriting TME research.
  5. Unpaired multi-omics remains open; existing methods (GLUE, Pamona) weaker than paired.
  6. SpatialData container is scverse's 2024 push, but AnnData remains core.
