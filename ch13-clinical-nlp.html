電子病歷與臨床 NLP

從 BioBERT 到 Med-Gemini——臨床 NLP 的 6 年躍進。

From BioBERT to Med-Gemini — six years of leaps in clinical NLP.

任務地景

臨床 NLP 的六種任務

🔖

① NER

從病歷文本抽診斷、藥物、檢查、症狀。i2b2 / n2c2 系列、ShARe/CLEF eHealth。

Extract diagnoses, drugs, tests, symptoms from notes. i2b2/n2c2, ShARe/CLEF eHealth.

🔗

② 概念規範化

把抽出來的 entity 對應到 UMLS / SNOMED CT / ICD-10。MetaMap, SciSpaCy, BioSyn。

Link entities to UMLS / SNOMED CT / ICD-10. MetaMap, SciSpaCy, BioSyn.

⏳

③ 時態與否定

"No fever" vs "Fever past 3 days" 對臨床推理至關重要。NegEx、Stanford temporalizer。

"No fever" vs "Fever past 3 days" — vital for clinical reasoning. NegEx, Stanford temporalizer.

📋

④ 文件摘要

住院摘要、轉診信、病程總結。MIMIC-IV BHC 數據集；Med-Gemini、GatorTronGPT 等做生成。

Discharge summary, referral letter, course narrative. MIMIC-IV BHC; Med-Gemini, GatorTronGPT.

❓

⑤ 醫學問答

MedQA、PubMedQA、MedMCQA、MMLU-clinical。Med-PaLM 2 達 86.5%、Med-Gemini 91.1%。

MedQA, PubMedQA, MedMCQA, MMLU-clinical. Med-PaLM 2: 86.5%; Med-Gemini: 91.1%.

📊

⑥ 結構化抽取

從敘事文本生成 OMOP CDM 結構化資料、預測 30-day 再入院、敗血症等。

Extract OMOP CDM from notes; predict 30-day readmission, sepsis, etc.

模型演進

進化階梯

年份模型關鍵MedQA
2019BioBERTPubMed + PMC 預訓 BERT~50%
2020PubMedBERT, ClinicalBERT從頭預訓於生醫~60%
2022GatorTron 8.9B (UF Health)第一個大型臨床 LLM~67%
2023Med-PaLM 2LLM 微調 + RLHF86.5%
2024Med-Gemini-L 1.0多模態 + reasoning91.1%
2024MedAlpaca / MEDITRON-70B開源臨床 LLM~76%
2024MedGemma (Google open)開源醫療 Gemma~80%
⚠️
2024 MIMIC-CDM 研究警示：儘管 LLM 在 MedQA 高分，在真實臨床決策（指引遵循、實驗室結果解讀）仍會犯系統性錯誤。不要把考試分數當臨床能力。 2024 MIMIC-CDM warning: despite high MedQA scores, LLMs still make systematic errors in real clinical decisions (guideline adherence, lab interpretation). Don't conflate exam scores with clinical competence.
實作

用 ClinicalBERT 抽診斷與藥物

from transformers import AutoTokenizer, AutoModelForTokenClassification, pipeline
ck = "alvaroalon2/biobert_diseases_ner"
tok = AutoTokenizer.from_pretrained(ck)
m   = AutoModelForTokenClassification.from_pretrained(ck)
ner = pipeline("ner", model=m, tokenizer=tok, aggregation_strategy="simple")
note = "55-yo male with type 2 diabetes presents with chest pain, on metformin 500 mg BID."
for e in ner(note):
    print(e["entity_group"], e["word"], round(e["score"],3))
library(reticulate); transformers <- import("transformers")
ck  <- "alvaroalon2/biobert_diseases_ner"
tok <- transformers$AutoTokenizer$from_pretrained(ck)
m   <- transformers$AutoModelForTokenClassification$from_pretrained(ck)
ner <- transformers$pipeline("ner", model=m, tokenizer=tok,
                            aggregation_strategy="simple")
note <- "55-yo male with type 2 diabetes presents with chest pain, on metformin 500 mg BID."
print(ner(note))
部署要點

把 LLM 放進醫院的 6 條原則

  1. 不直接生成臨床決定：LLM 草擬，臨床醫師簽署。
  2. No autonomous decisions: LLM drafts; clinician signs.
  3. RAG 接知識庫：UpToDate、ICD、藥典都接外部 retrieval（見 Ch16）。
  4. Connect via RAG: UpToDate, ICD, formulary via retrieval (Ch16).
  5. 本地 / 私有雲部署：MedGemma / MEDITRON 開源權重可上 NVIDIA NIM、vLLM。
  6. On-prem / private cloud: open weights (MedGemma, MEDITRON) on NVIDIA NIM, vLLM.
  7. 幻覺監控：對輸出做 self-check 與 retrieval-grounded 比對。
  8. Hallucination monitoring: self-check & grounded comparison.
  9. 子族表現審核：定期檢查跨族裔、性別、年齡層 AUC（見 Ch04 / Ch17）。
  10. Subgroup audit: monitor AUC across race/gender/age (Ch04/Ch17).
  11. 差分隱私 / 聯邦學習：訓練端不暴露 PHI。
  12. DP / FL: keep PHI off the training side.

1. 目前在 USMLE MedQA 拿到最高分的模型是？

1. The model with the highest MedQA (USMLE) score is?

A. BioBERT
B. ClinicalBERT
C. Med-PaLM 2
D. Med-Gemini-L 1.0 (91.1%)
D 正解。Nature Medicine 2024 報告。D. Nature Medicine 2024.

2. 2024 MIMIC-CDM 研究的核心警示是？

2. The 2024 MIMIC-CDM warning is?

A. 高 MedQA 分數的 LLM 在實際臨床仍系統性犯錯A. High-MedQA LLMs still make systematic errors in real clinical decisions
B. LLM 完全不能用
C. MIMIC 不再可用
D. RLHF 沒效
A 正解。考試 ≠ 臨床。A. Exams ≠ clinic.

3. 臨床 NLP 為什麼必須處理「否定」？

3. Why must clinical NLP handle negation?

A. 文法美觀
B. "no fever" 跟 "fever" 的臨床意涵完全相反B. "no fever" and "fever" mean opposite clinical things
C. 加快推論
D. 法規要求
B 正解。否定錯誤是臨床 NLP 最嚴重的 false call 來源。B. Negation errors are the worst false-call source in clinical NLP.
