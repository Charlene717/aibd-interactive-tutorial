  • 知道 LoRA / QLoRA / full fine-tune / RAG 各自適合什麼情境
  • 能規劃 PHI 去識別化 + on-premise 部署 + subgroup audit 三大臨床落地檢核點

Whether you're a clinician new to NLP, a data scientist planning deployment, or a PI trying to understand why Med-Gemini scored 91.1% on MedQA, this is your entry point. After reading you'll be able to:

  • Distinguish BioBERT, ClinicalBERT, GatorTron, Med-PaLM 2, Med-Gemini, MedGemma and place them on a timeline

MedQA, PubMedQA, MedMCQA, MMLU-clinical. Med-PaLM 2: 86.5%; Med-Gemini: 91.1%.

什麼是「臨床 LLM」？三層說明

🌱 新手版本

一個讀過很多醫療文本（PubMed、UpToDate、病歷）的 ChatGPT，能用醫療詞彙跟你對話、回答醫學考題、抽病歷重點。但不能取代醫師做臨床決定。

A ChatGPT-like model that has read lots of medical text (PubMed, UpToDate, notes); it can chat in medical vocabulary, answer exam questions, summarize charts — but cannot replace clinical decisions.

🎓 中階版本

在大量無標註醫療文本上 self-supervised 預訓，再經 SFT (supervised fine-tune) + RLHF 對齊醫療任務。代表：BioBERT (2019)、ClinicalBERT (2020)、GatorTron (2022)、Med-PaLM 2 (2023)、Med-Gemini (2024)、MedGemma (2024)。

Self-supervised pretrain on huge unlabeled medical text, then SFT + RLHF aligns to clinical tasks. Exemplars: BioBERT (2019), ClinicalBERT (2020), GatorTron (2022), Med-PaLM 2 (2023), Med-Gemini (2024), MedGemma (2024).

🔬 進階版本

符合 Bommasani Foundation Model 四特性（scale + self-supervision + generality + emergence），同時：(a) 對 PHI 經過 Safe Harbor / Limited Dataset 處理；(b) 透過 LoRA / QLoRA / Adapter 做 parameter-efficient fine-tune；(c) 連 RAG 與 tool-use；(d) 對 ICD-10、SNOMED、HPO、UMLS 等 ontology 有 entity linking 能力。Med-Gemini-L 1.0 是首個多模態整合此能力的旗艦。

Meets Bommasani's 4 FM properties (scale + self-supervision + generality + emergence), and: (a) PHI processed via Safe Harbor / Limited Dataset; (b) LoRA/QLoRA/Adapter for parameter-efficient FT; (c) connected to RAG & tool-use; (d) entity-linked to ICD-10, SNOMED, HPO, UMLS. Med-Gemini-L 1.0 is the flagship multimodal integration.
