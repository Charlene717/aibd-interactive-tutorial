觀念階梯

什麼是 Dice 分數？三層說明

🌱 新手版本

Dice 就是「AI 畫的範圍 與 醫師畫的範圍，重疊面積佔兩者面積的比例」。1.0 = 完全一樣，0 = 完全沒重疊。一般 ≥0.8 就算「臨床可用」。

Dice = "how much of the AI's region overlaps the clinician's region, as a fraction of their combined area." 1.0 = perfect match, 0 = no overlap. ≥0.8 is generally "clinically usable".

🎓 中階版本

數學上 Dice = 2·|A∩B| / (|A|+|B|)；與 IoU 關係 Dice = 2·IoU / (1+IoU)。對類別不平衡（小目標）更穩定，因為它兩邊都正規化。Dice loss = 1 − Dice，是 BCE 在腫瘤分割上的常見替代。

Mathematically Dice = 2·|A∩B| / (|A|+|B|); relates to IoU as Dice = 2·IoU / (1+IoU). More stable than accuracy under class imbalance (small targets) because it normalizes both sides. Dice loss = 1 − Dice, a common BCE substitute for tumor segmentation.

🔬 進階版本

Dice 對「邊界誤差」不敏感、對「漏分割中央」過敏 → 臨床常配 Hausdorff distance 95% 與 Surface Dice（Nikolov 2021）報告。新算法 (e.g. boundary-Dice、cIDice) 在 2024 開始流行。Dice 也不單調 → 訓練時常用 generalised Dice + cross-entropy 混合損失（Sudre 2017）。

Dice is insensitive to boundary error but oversensitive to missed centers → clinical reports pair it with Hausdorff distance 95% and Surface Dice (Nikolov 2021). New variants (boundary-Dice, cIDice) gained traction in 2024. Dice is non-monotonic → training often uses generalised Dice + cross-entropy hybrid loss (Sudre 2017).

案例故事

兩個改變放射科的故事

2017-11 · CheXNet 上線

史丹佛兩個學生，用 14 萬張 X 光打敗放射科主治

2017 年 11 月，Pranav Rajpurkar 與 Jeremy Irvin 兩位 Stanford 學生，用 NIH ChestX-ray14（112,120 張前後位胸 X 光、14 類標籤）訓練一個 121 層 DenseNet，命名為 CheXNet。他們把 420 張測試影像同時給 CheXNet 和 4 位 Stanford 主治放射師看。

結果：CheXNet F1 = 0.435，4 位放射師平均 F1 = 0.387。AI 第一次在實質性、可比較的放射任務上「超越」人類專家。論文預印僅僅 4 天就刷爆 Twitter，The Verge / NYT 隔週跟進。但 Stanford 團隊很快補充：「我們的標籤本身是用 NLP 從報告抽的，有 5–10% 雜訊；且測試只看 X 光，醫師臨床上看更多脈絡。」

這故事的真正啟示不是「AI 贏」，而是「14 萬張公開資料 + 121 層 CNN + 兩個學生 = 主治級表現」，意味著醫療 AI 的入門門檻從此完全改變。CheXNet GitHub 至今星 1.5K，是無數放射科 AI demo 的起點。

In November 2017, two Stanford students — Pranav Rajpurkar and Jeremy Irvin — trained a 121-layer DenseNet, dubbed CheXNet, on NIH ChestX-ray14 (112,120 frontal chest X-rays, 14 labels). They tested 420 held-out images against 4 Stanford attending radiologists.

Result: CheXNet F1 = 0.435 vs. radiologists' average F1 = 0.387. The first time an AI beat human experts on a substantive, comparable radiology task. The preprint blew up on Twitter in 4 days; The Verge / NYT followed within a week. Stanford added caveats: "Our labels were NLP-extracted from reports with 5–10% noise, and clinicians normally see far more context than just the X-ray."

The real lesson isn't "AI wins" — it's that "140K public images + 121-layer CNN + two students = attending-grade performance". The entry bar for medical AI changed forever. CheXNet's GitHub repo still has 1.5K stars and is the starting point for countless radiology AI demos.

教訓：公開資料集 + 標準 CNN = 整個領域的「門檻基線」。任何新模型必須先打贏 CheXNet。
Lesson: a public dataset + a standard CNN sets the field's "baseline floor". Every new model must first beat CheXNet.
2024-03 · 一個放射科部署 MedSAM

一位主治：把 MedSAM 接進 PACS 的 60 天

2024 年 3 月，台北榮總影像醫學部一位主治看完 MedSAM 論文（Ma et al., Nat Comm 2024）後，決定不寫程式碼導入它。整個流程是：

  1. Day 1–5：影像研究室 IT 把 MedSAM 權重下載 (~400 MB)，部署到內部 GPU server（一張 RTX A6000，48GB）。
  2. Day 6–15：用 OHIF Viewer (DICOM web viewer) + 自製 plugin，讓主治在 PACS 中能「點一下病灶 → 自動分割」。
  3. Day 16–40：三位主治用 200 張臨床 CT（含肝臟轉移、肺結節、淋巴結）做 inter-rater 校正，發現對「邊界清晰的轉移瘤」Dice 0.91，「邊界模糊的肝細胞癌」Dice 0.68（需手動修正）。
  4. Day 41–60：計算實質工時節省：原本每例 RECIST 量測 12 分鐘 → 用 MedSAM 後 4.5 分鐘（節省 62.5%）。但仍有 7% 例子主治會完全棄用 AI 結果重畫。

主治的結論：「不是 AI 取代我，是 AI 把『畫圈圈』這 12 分鐘還給我，讓我去看更多複雜的個案。」這正是 FDA PCCP 強調的 human-in-the-loop 部署典範。

March 2024: an attending at Taipei VGH's radiology department reads the MedSAM paper (Ma et al., Nat Comm 2024) and decides to deploy it without writing code. The recipe:

  1. Days 1–5: Imaging IT downloads MedSAM weights (~400 MB) to an internal GPU server (one RTX A6000, 48GB).
  2. Days 6–15: OHIF Viewer (DICOM web viewer) + a custom plugin let attendings "click a lesion → auto-segment" inside PACS.
  3. Days 16–40: Three attendings calibrate on 200 clinical CTs (liver mets, lung nodules, lymph nodes). For sharply bounded mets Dice = 0.91; for fuzzy HCC Dice = 0.68 (needs manual correction).
  4. Days 41–60: Time savings: 12 min/case for RECIST measurement → 4.5 min with MedSAM (62.5% saved). But 7% of cases still get discarded and re-drawn from scratch.

The attending's takeaway: "AI didn't replace me — it gave back the 12 minutes I spent drawing circles, so I could look at more complex cases." This is the human-in-the-loop deployment pattern FDA PCCP champions.

教訓：影像 AI 的真正價值不是「全自動」，而是「把醫師的時間從機械任務還給判斷任務」。
Lesson: imaging AI's real value isn't "fully automatic" — it's "moving clinicians from mechanical to judgment tasks".

想再深一點？三個機制細節

(1) U-Net skip connection 為什麼至關重要？

U-Net (Ronneberger 2015) 是「encoder–decoder」對稱結構：encoder 透過 max-pooling 把空間維度從 572×572 縮到 28×28（高語義、低解析）；decoder 再用 up-conv 恢復。問題是：down-sampling 會丟失邊界訊息，醫療影像最在乎的就是邊界（腫瘤邊緣 = 切除範圍）。

Skip connection 把 encoder 各層的高解析特徵直接拼接到對應 decoder 層，公式是：xdec(l) = UpConv(xdec(l+1)) ⊕ xenc(l)。這讓 decoder 同時擁有「全局語義」與「局部細節」，避免邊界模糊。2019 之後幾乎所有醫療分割架構（V-Net, Attention U-Net, TransUNet, Swin UNETR）都保留這個 motif。

Ablation 顯示：拿掉 skip connection 後 Dice 下降 5–15pp（依任務），是 U-Net 「最重要的單一設計」。

U-Net (Ronneberger 2015) is an "encoder–decoder" symmetric net: encoder shrinks spatial size from 572×572 to 28×28 via max-pooling (high-level semantics, low resolution); decoder restores via up-conv. Problem: down-sampling discards boundary information, but boundaries are exactly what medical imaging cares about (tumor margin = resection extent).

Skip connections concatenate each encoder layer's high-resolution features directly to the matching decoder layer: xdec(l) = UpConv(xdec(l+1)) ⊕ xenc(l). The decoder thus gets global semantics + local detail, avoiding blurry boundaries. Almost every post-2019 medical segmentation arch (V-Net, Attention U-Net, TransUNet, Swin UNETR) keeps this motif.

Ablations show: removing skip connections drops Dice 5–15pp depending on task — the "single most important design choice" in U-Net.

(2) Dice Loss 為什麼勝過 BCE？

Binary Cross-Entropy 對每個 voxel 獨立計算錯誤；在腫瘤分割「前景僅 0.5%」時，模型只要把所有 voxel 都判成背景，BCE 就可以低到 0.005。準確率 99.5%，但 Dice = 0。

Dice loss = 1 − (2·Σp·g + ε) / (Σp + Σg + ε)，p 是預測機率、g 是 ground truth。正規化兩邊面積後，分母自動反映類別不平衡——小目標的梯度被「放大」到與大目標相當。Milletari 2016（V-Net）首次提出，現已是 3D 醫療分割預設。但 Dice loss 在「前景 = 0」時梯度為零 → 通常與 BCE 線性組合：L = α·Dice + (1−α)·BCE，α≈0.5–0.7。

Binary Cross-Entropy treats each voxel independently; for a tumor with 0.5% foreground, predicting "all background" gives BCE ≈ 0.005 — 99.5% accuracy, Dice 0.

Dice loss = 1 − (2·Σp·g + ε) / (Σp + Σg + ε), where p is predicted probability and g is ground truth. Normalizing both sides automatically reflects class imbalance — small-target gradients get "amplified" to the scale of large targets. Milletari 2016 (V-Net) introduced it; now default for 3D medical segmentation. Caveat: Dice loss has zero gradient when the foreground is empty → typically combined linearly with BCE: L = α·Dice + (1−α)·BCE, α ≈ 0.5–0.7.

(3) 3D nnU-Net 的「自配置」是怎麼自動的？

nnU-Net 的核心是一套「從資料指紋產生超參數」的規則：讀進 NIfTI 資料集後，自動計算 dataset fingerprint（影像大小中位數、voxel spacing 分布、強度分布、類別比例、組織 vs 背景比）。再用工程化規則映射到：

  • Plan A：2D U-Net（用於 anisotropic 高度 slice）；Plan B：3D full-res（GPU memory 內塞得下時）；Plan C：3D low-res → cascade（影像太大時兩階段）。
  • Patch size + batch size：自動最大化在 11–24GB GPU 內可訓練的 patch，遵守「GPU 占用 80%」啟發式。
  • Pre-processing：CT 用 [0.5, 99.5] percentile + Z-score；MRI 直接 Z-score；resample 到中位 voxel spacing。
  • Training：500 epochs × 250 iter，SGD + 0.99 nesterov，poly LR decay；DiceCE loss + 強增強（rotation/scaling/gamma/noise）。

2024 版的 nnU-Net Revisited 加入 ResEncL（ResNet 風格 encoder）與 大 patch 模式，在 Decathlon / KiTS / BraTS 不少子任務再進一步。重點是 「沒有任何超參數需要手調，open-box 又是 SOTA」。

nnU-Net's core is a rule-set that derives hyperparameters from a dataset fingerprint. After ingesting a NIfTI dataset it computes fingerprint (median image size, voxel spacing distribution, intensity distribution, class ratios, tissue:background ratio), then engineering rules map this to:

  • Plan A: 2D U-Net (anisotropic high slices); Plan B: 3D full-res (when GPU memory permits); Plan C: 3D low-res → cascade (for huge volumes).
  • Patch + batch size: auto-maximize for 11–24GB GPU under an 80%-occupancy heuristic.
  • Pre-processing: CT uses [0.5, 99.5]-percentile + Z-score; MRI Z-score directly; resample to median voxel spacing.
  • Training: 500 epochs × 250 iters, SGD + 0.99 Nesterov, poly LR decay; DiceCE loss + strong augmentation (rotation/scaling/gamma/noise).

The 2024 nnU-Net Revisited adds ResEncL (ResNet-style encoder) and large-patch mode, further improving Decathlon/KiTS/BraTS subtasks. Bottom line: no hyperparameter tuning needed — open-box yet SOTA.

迷思 vs 事實

放射科 AI 的 5 個誤會

① 5 年內 AI 將取代放射科醫師。
① Hinton 2016 預測已被反駁；放射科招生不減反增。
Hinton 2016 說「我們應停止訓練放射科」，但 2024 ACGME 統計顯示放射科住院醫總員額連 8 年上升。AI 已普及 triage / 量測，但臨床判斷、跨科溝通、罕病鑑別仍由人主導；FDA 1451 個 AI 醫材沒一個是「全自主決策」。 Hinton said in 2016 "we should stop training radiologists" — but the 2024 ACGME report shows radiology residency slots have grown 8 years in a row. AI is mainstream for triage and measurement, but clinical judgment, cross-specialty communication, rare disease workups stay with humans; none of FDA's 1451 AI devices is fully autonomous.
② nnU-Net 已被 Transformer 取代。
② 2024 nnU-Net Revisited 證明：CNN+nnU-Net 仍是 3D SOTA。
Isensee 2024 在 BraTS、KiTS、Decathlon 多項任務上重新比較 SwinUNETR、UNETR 等 Transformer 變體，CNN-based U-Net + nnU-Net 自動配置依然贏。Transformer 在 patch 之間建關係，但 3D 醫療影像的「局部紋理 + 形狀」更適合卷積的歸納偏置。 Isensee 2024 re-benchmarked SwinUNETR, UNETR, etc. on BraTS, KiTS, Decathlon — CNN U-Net + nnU-Net auto-config still wins. Transformers excel at long-range patch relations, but 3D medical imaging's "local texture + shape" maps better to conv inductive bias.
③ MedSAM 可以零訓練應對所有任務。
③ 邊界模糊 / 罕模態仍要 fine-tune。
MedSAM 在「自然邊界清晰」（器官、明顯腫瘤）零樣本 Dice 0.85+，但在 ultrasound, OCT, microscopy 等與訓練分佈差距大的模態，Dice 可掉到 0.5 以下。建議：先 zero-shot 評估 → 視情形 LoRA fine-tune 5K samples 即可大幅回升。 MedSAM hits zero-shot Dice 0.85+ on sharp natural boundaries (organs, clear tumors), but drops below 0.5 on ultrasound, OCT, microscopy (out-of-distribution). Rule of thumb: zero-shot test first → LoRA fine-tune with 5K samples if needed.
④ Dice 越高 = 臨床越有用。
④ 邊界誤差才反映手術可用性。
Dice 0.92 但 Hausdorff 95% 是 25 mm（邊界系統性偏移 25 mm），對放射治療 GTV/CTV 不可用。臨床部署需同時報告 Dice + HD95 + Surface Dice（Nikolov 2021 DeepMind eye/head-neck 標竿）。 Dice 0.92 yet HD95 = 25 mm (25 mm systematic boundary offset) is useless for radiotherapy GTV/CTV. Clinical deployment requires reporting Dice + HD95 + Surface Dice (Nikolov 2021 DeepMind eye/head-neck benchmark).
⑤ 台灣 TFDA 流程比美 FDA 慢得多。
⑤ 2024 TFDA SaMD 路徑大幅優化，中位 3.4 個月。
TFDA 2023 推出「AI/ML 軟體醫療器材分流指引」，第二類 SaMD 中位審查 3.4 個月，僅略長於 FDA 510(k) 的 ~3 個月。實際案例：行動智抑（中華電信）、Taimedimg AI（行動心電）、雲象 aetherAI 病理多項已取證。 TFDA's 2023 "AI/ML SaMD triage guidance" cut Class-II SaMD review to median 3.4 months — only slightly longer than FDA 510(k)'s ~3 months. Cleared examples: Chunghwa Telecom MindFlow, Taimedimg AI ECG, aetherAI pathology.

陷阱

8 大常見陷阱

  1. DICOM 隱資訊洩漏：scanner ID、protocol、機構等成「捷徑特徵」，造成跨機構表現崩潰。
  2. DICOM metadata leakage: scanner ID, protocol, institution as shortcut features → cross-institution performance collapse.
  3. Spacing / orientation 未統一：3D 重採樣到固定 voxel spacing 是基本動作。
  4. Inconsistent spacing/orientation: resample 3D to fixed voxel spacing — must.
  5. Class imbalance：罕見器官 / 病灶 1% 也常見，需 Dice + Focal + 加權取樣。
  6. Class imbalance: rare organs/lesions ~1% — use Dice + Focal + weighted sampling.
  7. Test-time augmentation：TTA 通常加 0.5–2 個 Dice，很值得。
  8. Test-time augmentation: TTA usually adds 0.5–2 Dice points.
  9. Mask 標註不一致：多 annotator 重疊區可用 SoftDice / 多標籤合併。
  10. Inconsistent masks: multi-annotator overlap → SoftDice or merge.
  11. Domain shift：CT vs MRI 跨模、不同醫院。Test-time adaptation 與 federated learning（Ch19）救命。
  12. Domain shift: CT vs MRI, different hospitals. TTA & federated learning (Ch19) help.
  13. Hidden labels via reports：直接從 radiology report 抽取「弱標籤」要當心 noise。
  14. Weak labels from reports: noisy supervision — handle with care.
  15. Reproducibility：固定種子、版本化 DICOM、log 環境，否則 cross-center 重現極難。
  16. Reproducibility: fix seeds, version DICOM, log env — cross-center reproducibility is otherwise unrealistic.
