案例

Obermeyer 2019：一個曾錯派 4400 萬美國患者照護的演算法

2019 年 Obermeyer 等人在 Science 公開一個美國醫療體系廣泛使用、影響約 2 億人的演算法：它用「未來醫療支出」作為「健康需求」的代理變數，結果系統性低估黑人病人的健康需求。在相同預測風險下，黑人病人實際病情比白人重得多。為什麼？因為非裔患者就醫機會與支出本就較低；演算法把「結構性歧視」誤學成「實際需求」。

這個案例是 AI 公平性的教科書級例子，它揭示：標籤選擇 (label choice) 本身可以是偏差來源，而不只是資料代表性的問題。

In 2019, Obermeyer et al. (Science) exposed an algorithm widely used across the US health system affecting ~200M people: it used "future health spending" as a proxy for "health need", systematically underestimating Black patients' needs. At the same predicted risk, Black patients were sicker than White patients. Why? Because African-American patients incur less spending due to structural barriers; the algorithm learned structural discrimination as if it were need.

This is the textbook case for AI fairness: label choice itself can be a source of bias, not just data representation.

🚨
教訓：「代理變數 (proxy)」是公平性最大的雷區。在生醫情境，常見錯誤代理包括「就醫次數」代表「疾病嚴重度」、「申報碼」代表「臨床診斷」、「自費比例」代表「健康風險」。 Lesson: Proxy variables are the biggest landmine for fairness. Frequent biomedical errors: using "visit counts" for "severity", "claim codes" for "clinical diagnosis", "out-of-pocket %" for "health risk".

公平性指標

七種你會在審查表上看到的公平性指標

① 統計獨立

各族群被預測為陽性的比例相同。最簡單但常與「真實疾病分布」衝突。

Positive prediction rate equal across groups. Simple, but often conflicts with true disease prevalence.

② 機會均等

真陽性率 (TPR / Sensitivity) 在各族群相同。臨床上常是首選。

TPR (sensitivity) equal across groups. Often the clinical preference.

③ 均等機率

TPR 和 FPR 都跨族群相同。比機會均等更嚴格。

Both TPR and FPR equal across groups. Stricter than equal opportunity.

④ 預測值同等

PPV / NPV 跨族群相同。臨床醫師最直覺。

PPV / NPV equal across groups. Most intuitive for clinicians.

⑤ 校準同等

各族群「預測機率 = 實際機率」。

Predicted probability = actual probability across groups.

⑥ 子族 AUC

各族群分別計算 AUROC / AUPRC。TRIPOD-AI 要求逐子群報告。

AUROC/AUPRC computed per group. TRIPOD-AI requires per-subgroup reporting.

⑦ 反事實公平

若交換敏感屬性（性別、族裔）預測不變。理論優雅，實作困難。

Prediction unchanged when sensitive attribute is flipped. Theoretically elegant, practically hard.

⚠️
不可能三角：除非各族群基期完全一致，否則「校準同等」「均等機率」「預測值同等」三者不能同時成立 (Chouldechova 2017)。臨床決策上要先和倫理委員會討論該守哪個。 Impossibility: Unless base rates are equal, "calibration", "equalized odds" and "predictive parity" cannot all hold simultaneously (Chouldechova 2017). Discuss with your ethics board which to prioritize.

案例故事

Epic Sepsis Model 撤回事件

2017-2021 · Epic ESM 部署與崩盤

從 170 家醫院部署到 BMJ 撕破臉

Epic 的 Sepsis Model (ESM) 是美國最大 EHR 廠商 Epic 在 2017 起內建的敗血症預警 AI——部署於 170+ 家美國醫院，影響數百萬病人。Epic 宣稱：AUC 0.76、sensitivity 0.76、能提早 8 小時警示。

但 Wong 等人（JAMA Intern Med 2021）對密西根大學的 38,455 名病人外部驗證：真實 AUC 只有 0.63；sensitivity 33%；PPV 12%。為了一次真陽性，醫師會被誤觸發 7 次假警報——這在臨床上叫「alert fatigue」，醫師最後乾脆關掉警報。

更嚴重的問題：Epic ESM 把「抗生素已開立」作為訓練特徵——但醫師開抗生素通常就是因為懷疑 sepsis，這形成了標籤洩漏。模型實際上學到「醫師已經想到 sepsis」，而不是「預測 sepsis」。

2022 Epic 推出修訂版 ESM v3，但已經失去信任。Bauer 等人在 BMJ 撰寫長文，呼籲所有 EHR AI 必須做跨機構外部驗證 + alert burden 評估才能上市。這啟發了 FDA PCCP 2024 終版指引納入 "real-world performance monitoring" 強制條款。

Epic's Sepsis Model (ESM) is the largest US EHR vendor's built-in sepsis-alert AI since 2017, deployed at 170+ US hospitals, affecting millions of patients. Epic claimed: AUC 0.76, sensitivity 0.76, 8-hour-early alerts.

But Wong et al. (JAMA Intern Med 2021) externally validated on 38,455 University of Michigan patients: real AUC only 0.63; sensitivity 33%; PPV 12%. For every true positive, doctors get 7 false alarms — clinically this is "alert fatigue", and clinicians eventually mute the warning entirely.

The deeper problem: Epic ESM included "antibiotics already ordered" as a feature — but doctors order antibiotics because they suspect sepsis. This is label leakage: the model learned "the doc already thought of sepsis", not "predict sepsis".

In 2022 Epic released ESM v3, but trust was gone. Bauer et al. (BMJ) called for mandatory cross-institutional external validation + alert burden assessment for all EHR AI. This shaped FDA's PCCP 2024 final guidance mandating real-world performance monitoring.

教訓：高 AUC + 沒做外部驗證 + 標籤洩漏 = 影響百萬病人的醫療誤導；監管必須跨越「廠商自驗」。
Lesson: high AUC + no external validation + label leakage = medical misguidance for millions; regulators must move beyond "vendor self-validation".
迷思 vs 事實

四個常見誤會

① 刪 18 項 = 完全去識別化。
① 仍可從高維資料重識別。
基因組與影像可重識別性更高（Gymrek 2013：STR 重複可定位姓氏）。Safe Harbor 是底線，需搭配 k-anonymity / differential privacy。Genomes and images are even more re-ID-able (Gymrek 2013: STRs can recover surnames). Safe Harbor is a floor; combine with k-anonymity / DP.
② Algorithmic fix 解決公平性。
② 標籤選擇本身是偏差源。
Obermeyer 2019 用「醫療支出」代理「健康需求」就是根本錯誤；換 loss function、加 fairness constraint 都無法修。需要回到「我們在預測什麼」的根本提問。Obermeyer 2019 used "spending" as a proxy for "need" — fundamental error. Changing loss or adding fairness constraints can't fix it. Step back to "what are we actually predicting".
③ FDA / EU 規範類似。
③ 邏輯完全不同。
EU 看 AI 應用情境的「風險等級」分四級（minimal / limited / high / unacceptable）；FDA 看醫材「類別 + intended use」（Class I/II/III）。同一個 AI 在 EU 可能高風險，在 FDA 是 510(k) exempt。EU classifies by application risk tier (minimal / limited / high / unacceptable); FDA by device class + intended use (I/II/III). The same AI may be EU-high-risk and FDA-510(k)-exempt.
④ PCCP = 無限更新。
④ 在預定協定內更新。
PCCP 三件套（modification description + protocol + impact assessment）必須具體；超出範圍（如改任務、改 population）必須重新 510(k) / PMA。FDA 2024-12 終版明確：bias mitigation + post-market surveillance 也是必備條款。PCCP triad (modification description + protocol + impact assessment) must be specific; outside-of-scope changes (new task, new population) require re-submission. FDA 2024-12 final: bias mitigation + post-market surveillance are mandatory.

想得更深

Q1. 為了公平性犧牲整體準確率，值得嗎？若 demographic parity 讓黑人受益但整體 AUC 下降 3pp，這 3pp 等於多少病人延誤診斷？這是純技術回答不了的價值判斷。參考：Pleiss 2017 NeurIPS 對 fairness-accuracy 權衡的形式化討論。
Q1. Is fairness worth a global accuracy hit?If demographic parity helps Black patients but drops global AUC 3pp, how many patients suffer delayed diagnosis? This is a value judgment beyond technique.Reference: Pleiss 2017 NeurIPS formalization of fairness-accuracy tradeoffs.
Q2. AI 犯錯誰負責？醫師、廠商還是 AI？FDA / EU 都把 final responsibility 放在「醫師 + 廠商」雙肩；但歐盟 2026 起新增 "AI Liability Directive"，使被害人提告更容易。台灣現況：尚無 AI 特定 liability，仍依民法 184 條侵權處理。
Q2. Who's liable when AI errs — physician, vendor, or AI?FDA / EU place final responsibility on physician + vendor jointly; from 2026 EU adds an "AI Liability Directive" easing victim lawsuits.Taiwan: no AI-specific liability yet; falls under Civil Code §184 torts.
Q3. 台灣 PIPA 與歐盟 GDPR、美國 HIPAA 的關鍵差異？PIPA 範圍最廣（含特種資料、敏感資料；不分醫療/商業）但執行較鬆；GDPR 罰款 4% 全球營收；HIPAA 只管 covered entity。行動：跨國研究務必同時做三套 DPIA。
Q3. Key differences between Taiwan PIPA, EU GDPR, US HIPAA?PIPA has the broadest scope (includes special and sensitive data; no domain restriction) but lighter enforcement; GDPR fines 4% global revenue; HIPAA covers only covered entities.Action: cross-border research needs all three DPIAs.
Q4. AI 醫材通過 FDA 後，可以多久不再驗證？FDA PCCP 要求年度 progress report + drift detection；但「實質性」變更（model retrain on new population）仍需重新提交。長期維護成本被嚴重低估。行業現況：很多新創公司燒完 grant 後就放生模型，FDA 2025 起加強稽核。
Q4. After FDA clearance, how long until re-validation?FDA PCCP mandates annual progress reports + drift detection; but "substantive" changes (retraining on new population) require resubmission. Long-term maintenance is severely underestimated.Industry: many startups abandon models after grant runs out; FDA tightening audits from 2025.
常見問答

5 個常見問題

TFDA 採用「分類管理」：(1) Class I 一般醫材（如電子體溫計式 AI）—登記制；(2) Class II 中度（如影像分析輔助）—簡易查驗；(3) Class III 高度（如自動診斷）—臨床試驗 + 完整查驗。2024 TFDA 發布「含 AI 軟體醫療器材技術性資料審查重點」對齊 FDA SaMD + IMDRF GMLP。台灣杉與台灣 AI Labs 已有聯邦學習平台供本地醫院使用。TFDA uses tiered classification: Class I (e.g., AI thermometer) – registration; Class II (e.g., imaging aid) – simplified review; Class III (e.g., autonomous diagnosis) – clinical trial + full review. 2024 TFDA released "AI-containing medical device technical review highlights" aligned with FDA SaMD + IMDRF GMLP. Taiwania + Taiwan AI Labs provide federated learning platforms for local hospitals.
8 項必備：(1) Intended use 明確人群 + 任務；(2) 訓練資料概述—來源、N、demographic；(3) 表現指標—整體 + 子族 AUC / calibration；(4) 外部驗證跨機構結果；(5) 限制—OOD、罕病、cohort drift；(6) 更新機制—PCCP 三件套；(7) 偏差緩解—子族審計與 mitigation；(8) 使用者警告—何時不該用此 AI。8 essentials: (1) Intended use — specific population + task; (2) Training data summary — source, N, demographics; (3) Performance — global + subgroup AUC/calibration; (4) External validation across institutions; (5) Limitations — OOD, rare disease, cohort drift; (6) Update mechanism — PCCP triad; (7) Bias mitigation — subgroup audits; (8) User warnings — when NOT to use this AI.
分三層：(1) 輔助文書（病歷草擬、信件）—OK，但需 PHI 處理 + 醫師最後簽字；(2) 輔助決策—需要 RAG + 引用追溯 + human-in-the-loop；(3) 自主診斷 / 處方—FDA / EU 均未核准任何純 LLM 做此。台灣 NHI 2025 規定院內 AI 文書必須註明 "AI 生成、由 [醫師] 審核"。Three tiers: (1) Document assistance (chart drafting, letters) — OK with PHI handling + final physician sign-off; (2) Decision support — needs RAG + citation tracing + human-in-loop; (3) Autonomous diagnosis / Rx — not approved by FDA / EU for any pure LLM. Taiwan NHI 2025 requires hospital AI documents to indicate "AI-generated, reviewed by [physician]".
看「傷害類型」決定：(1) diagnostic AI（過診 vs 漏診）—Equal Opportunity（TPR 跨族群相同）；(2) resource allocation（如腎臟移植）—Demographic Parity（分配比例相同）；(3) risk scoring—Calibration（同一分數對應相同實際風險）。Chouldechova 三者不能同時——和倫理委員會討論優先順序。Depends on harm type: (1) diagnostic AI (over/under-diagnosis) — Equal Opportunity (equal TPR); (2) resource allocation (e.g., transplant) — Demographic Parity (equal allocation); (3) risk scoring — Calibration (same score = same actual risk). Chouldechova: can't have all three — discuss priority with ethics board.

US Census 2020 used ε=19.61. Biomedical recommended: ε=4-8, δ=1e-5, monitor long-term budget via Privacy Loss Distribution.
