主張

方向反轉：90% 計算 → 10% 實驗

過去 20 年生醫研究的常態是：90% 實驗 + 10% 計算。AlphaFold 3、scGPT、Med-Gemini 預示一個對立的未來：90% 計算先驗、10% 關鍵實驗驗證。Chan Zuckerberg Initiative 2025 推出的 rBio（第一個在 Virtual Cell 模擬上做推理的 AI）就是這個方向的代表。

這個範式如果落地，會壓縮藥物 R&D 時間從 12 年降到 4-5 年、把基礎研究的「假設 → 實驗」循環從週縮成天。

For 20 years, biomedicine has been 90% wet-lab + 10% compute. AlphaFold 3, scGPT, Med-Gemini herald the inverse: 90% computational prior, 10% key experiments. CZI's 2025 rBio — the first AI trained to reason about cellular biology via virtual-cell simulations — exemplifies this direction.

If this paradigm lands, drug R&D could compress from 12 years to 4–5; basic-research "hypothesis → experiment" cycles drop from weeks to days.

案例故事
2025 · CZI rBio 啟動

rBio：第一個在 virtual cell 推理的 AI

2025 年 11 月，Chan Zuckerberg Initiative 公布 rBio——第一個在「virtual cell 模擬」上做推理的 AI 模型。它不靠真實 perturb-seq 資料訓練，而是先在數百萬個 simulated cell 上學習，然後 generalize 到真實實驗。

關鍵架構：(1) Virtual cell simulator—整合 scGPT + ODE 動力學 + 化學知識庫；(2) rBio 推理模型—在 simulator 上做 in-silico knockout、drug screening、cell type transition 預測；(3) 真實實驗驗證循環—rBio 預測 → 濕實驗驗證 → 反饋給 simulator 校準。

同時 Biohub 投入 $500M Virtual Biology Initiative；CZI + NVIDIA 推 Virtual Cell Platform；2025 Recursion + Exscientia 合併成立首個 AI-drug self-driving lab。這四個事件加起來，標誌著生醫研究典範轉移正式開始：從「跑實驗 + 偶爾算」變成「先算 + 關鍵實驗驗證」。

但謹慎之處：(1) virtual cell 還很初級，無法模擬複雜表型；(2) rBio 對「未見」化合物泛化仍弱；(3) 生物複雜性可能限制這個典範的天花板。我們在「ImageNet 時刻」之後 14 年才看到 AlphaFold 2；virtual cell 的「ImageNet 時刻」可能也需要 5-10 年。

In November 2025, Chan Zuckerberg Initiative announced rBio — the first AI model trained to reason on "virtual cell simulations". Instead of training on real perturb-seq data, it first learns from millions of simulated cells, then generalizes to real experiments.

Architecture: (1) Virtual cell simulator — combines scGPT + ODE kinetics + chemical knowledge base; (2) rBio reasoning model — does in-silico knockouts, drug screening, cell-type transitions on the simulator; (3) real-experiment validation loop — rBio predicts → wet-lab tests → feedback to simulator for calibration.

Simultaneously, Biohub committed $500M to the Virtual Biology Initiative; CZI + NVIDIA launched the Virtual Cell Platform; 2025 Recursion + Exscientia merged into the first AI-drug self-driving lab. These four events together mark the official start of a biomedical paradigm shift: from "run experiments + occasionally compute" to "compute first + key experiments validate".

But caution: (1) virtual cells are still primitive — can't simulate complex phenotypes; (2) rBio generalizes poorly to unseen compounds; (3) biological complexity may cap this paradigm's ceiling. We saw AlphaFold 2 14 years after the ImageNet moment; virtual cells may need another 5-10 years for their own.

教訓：2025 是「virtual biology 元年」；2030 可能是「AI scientist 元年」。準備好參與這場變革。
Lesson: 2025 is "Year One of virtual biology"; 2030 may be "Year One of AI Scientist". Get ready to participate.

Q1. 「AI Scientist」真的可能嗎？Sakana AI 2024 試行讓 AI 自主寫 NeurIPS workshop 論文，結果引發 ELSI 爭議（同行評審該怎麼做）。2030 前可能達到 AI 助研究員水平；2040 前 AI 全自主發現仍困難。辯論：科學的 inductive leap 能完全演算法化嗎？
Q1. Is "AI Scientist" really possible?Sakana AI 2024 tried having AI autonomously write NeurIPS workshop papers — triggering ELSI debate (how do peer reviewers handle it?). By 2030, AI may reach research-assistant level; full-autonomous discovery before 2040 is still hard.Debate: can scientific inductive leaps be fully algorithmized?
Q2. 台灣 / 亞洲在這場革命的角色？三條路：(1) 應用層—在地化 fine-tune Foundation Models；(2) 資料層—Taiwan Biobank、BBJ 提供亞洲代表性；(3) 基礎研究—中研院、台大、清華、陽明參與 FM 開發。機會：在 LMIC + 亞洲族裔 AI 醫療上建立全球領導力。
Q2. Taiwan / Asia's role in this revolution?Three paths: (1) application layer — localized fine-tuning of FMs; (2) data layer — Taiwan Biobank, BBJ for Asian representation; (3) basic research — Academia Sinica, NTU, NTHU, NYCU contribute to FM development.Opportunity: build global leadership in LMIC + Asian-ancestry medical AI.
Q3. 醫師會被 AI 取代嗎？不是「取代」，是「重組」。WHO 2024 預期未來 10 年醫師工作量分布變化：影像 triage、文獻摘要、初步差異診斷 → 自動化；臨床判斷、病人溝通、實驗設計、倫理拿捏 → 仍由人主導。教育：醫學院須加入 AI literacy 必修課；NMC 2024 起新指引。
Q3. Will doctors be replaced by AI?Not "replaced" — reorganized. WHO 2024 expects next-10-year workload redistribution: imaging triage, lit summaries, initial DDx → automated; clinical judgment, communication, experimental design, ethics → still humans.Education: medical schools must add AI literacy as core; NMC 2024 new guidelines.
Q4. AI 鴻溝會擴大嗎？高收入國家累積 80% 訓練資料；模型偏向他們的人口。LMIC 怎麼參與？聯邦學習 + open-weights + ancestry-diverse cohort 是希望，但需要全球 governance。參考：WHO 2024 AI Ethics for Health 報告。
Q4. Will the AI divide widen?HICs hold 80% training data; models skew to their populations. How do LMICs participate? Federated learning + open weights + ancestry-diverse cohorts are hope, but need global governance.Reference: WHO 2024 AI Ethics for Health.
