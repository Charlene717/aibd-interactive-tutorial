未來：AI 帶來的範式轉移

Virtual Cell、self-driving labs、digital twins、AI-enabled trials——下一個十年的地圖。

Virtual Cell, self-driving labs, digital twins, AI-enabled trials — the map of the next decade.

主張

方向反轉：90% 計算 → 10% 實驗

過去 20 年生醫研究的常態是：90% 實驗 + 10% 計算。AlphaFold 3、scGPT、Med-Gemini 預示一個對立的未來：90% 計算先驗、10% 關鍵實驗驗證。Chan Zuckerberg Initiative 2025 推出的 rBio（第一個在 Virtual Cell 模擬上做推理的 AI）就是這個方向的代表。

這個範式如果落地，會壓縮藥物 R&D 時間從 12 年降到 4-5 年、把基礎研究的「假設 → 實驗」循環從週縮成天。

For 20 years, biomedicine has been 90% wet-lab + 10% compute. AlphaFold 3, scGPT, Med-Gemini herald the inverse: 90% computational prior, 10% key experiments. CZI's 2025 rBio — the first AI trained to reason about cellular biology via virtual-cell simulations — exemplifies this direction.

If this paradigm lands, drug R&D could compress from 12 years to 4–5; basic-research "hypothesis → experiment" cycles drop from weeks to days.

六大趨勢

2026 之後可能發生的事

🧫

① Virtual Cell 興起

CZI Virtual Cells Platform + NVIDIA 合作、Biohub $500M Virtual Biology Initiative、Billion Cells Project (2025)。目標：能在電腦中「執行」一個完整細胞。

CZI Virtual Cells Platform + NVIDIA collaboration; Biohub's $500M Virtual Biology Initiative; Billion Cells Project (2025). Goal: simulate a complete cell in silico.

🤖

② 自駕實驗室普及

Recursion + Exscientia 合併、Aspuru-Guzik、Carnegie Mellon Cloud Lab。從化學擴展到細胞、基因編輯、抗體最佳化。

Recursion + Exscientia merger; Aspuru-Guzik; CMU Cloud Lab. Extends from chemistry to cells, gene editing, antibody optimization.

👤

③ 患者 Digital Twin

結合基因體 + EHR + 影像 + 穿戴的個人化模擬人。GE / Siemens / Unlearn.AI 已在 RCT 與心衰中試行；未來十年可能成為臨床決策標配。

Personalized simulators combining genomics + EHR + imaging + wearables. GE / Siemens / Unlearn.AI piloted in RCTs and heart failure; could become a clinical standard in the next decade.

🧪

④ AI 增強的臨床試驗

External control arms（外部對照組）、synthetic control、自動病人匹配。FDA / EMA 已開放 RWD/RWE 框架。

External control arms, synthetic controls, automated trial matching. FDA / EMA have opened RWD/RWE frameworks.

🧬

⑤ 從序列 / 結構走向「功能」

Evo、Geneformer 2、ESM-3 都嘗試把「功能」變成第一級輸出而非附帶。設計能執行特定生物功能的蛋白與基因迴路成為主流目標。

Evo, Geneformer 2, ESM-3 try to make "function" a first-class output. Designing proteins/circuits to perform specific biological functions becomes the mainline target.

🏛️

⑥ 監管追上

EU AI Act 高風險條款 2026-08 全面生效、FDA PCCP 落地、各國衛福部開始要求公平性審計與後市監測。AI 醫療進入「可審計、可追責」階段。

EU AI Act high-risk rules fully apply 2026-08; FDA PCCP operational; ministries worldwide require fairness audits & post-market monitoring. AI medicine enters "auditable, accountable" era.

開放問題

十年內待解

  1. Foundation Models 對「分布外」生物現象的歸納能力到底有多強？
  2. How well do foundation models generalize to "out-of-distribution" biological phenomena?
  3. 「Causal」AI 何時能從相關性走到反事實干預？
  4. When will "causal" AI move from correlation to counterfactual intervention?
  5. 在強隱私約束下，FL+DP 能不能達到「機構間共享 OMOP」的同等成效？
  6. Can FL+DP match centralized OMOP-sharing under strict privacy?
  7. 數十億參數的醫療 LLM 是否真的比中型專科 LLM + RAG 更有臨床價值？
  8. Are billion-parameter medical LLMs really more clinically valuable than mid-size specialty LLM + RAG?
  9. AI 介入後，醫師的「臨床決策技能去技能化」會發生嗎？怎麼緩解？
  10. Will AI cause "deskilling" of clinicians? How do we mitigate?
  11. 如何讓資源有限地區（LMICs）獲得 Foundation Model 紅利，避免「AI 鴻溝」？
  12. How do we ensure low-resource regions (LMICs) share the foundation-model dividend and avoid an "AI divide"?
