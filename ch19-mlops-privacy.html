MLOps、隱私與聯邦學習

把模型穩定送進臨床——MLflow、ONNX、BentoML、Federated Learning、Differential Privacy 全套。

Deliver models to the clinic reliably — MLflow, ONNX, BentoML, federated learning, differential privacy.

新手友善 中階 進階補充

這一章你會學到什麼？

從剛訓好第一個模型不知如何部署到準備跨機構聯邦研究：

  • 串起 MLOps 五階段：data versioning → tracking → packaging → monitoring → retraining
  • 分辨 De-id / DP / Federated Learning / SMPC / HE 五種隱私技術
  • 讀懂 ε-δ Differential Privacy 與 Sweeney 87% 重識別案例
  • 避開「刪 PHI 18 項 = 安全」「FL 完全免風險」迷思

From just trained a model, don't know how to deploy to preparing cross-institution federated research:

  • Chain MLOps 5 stages: data versioning → tracking → packaging → monitoring → retraining
  • Differentiate De-id / DP / Federated Learning / SMPC / HE
  • Understand ε-δ DP and Sweeney's 87% re-identification case
  • Steer clear of "remove 18 PHI = safe" and "FL is risk-free" myths
MLOps

生醫 MLOps 的五階段

資料版本化

DVC / lakeFS / Pachyderm；DICOM、FASTQ、AnnData 都需要可重現的版本。

DVC / lakeFS / Pachyderm; DICOM, FASTQ, AnnData need reproducible versioning.

實驗追蹤

MLflow / Weights & Biases / Neptune；每次 run 記錄超參、metric、artifact、commit。

MLflow / W&B / Neptune; log hyperparams, metrics, artifacts, commits per run.

模型打包與部署

ONNX 跨框架；BentoML / Triton / vLLM 服務化；Docker / Singularity 環境鎖定。

ONNX for cross-framework; BentoML / Triton / vLLM for serving; Docker / Singularity for environment lock.

監控與漂移偵測

Evidently / Arize / WhyLogs 偵測 data drift、prediction drift、子族表現衰退。

Evidently / Arize / WhyLogs detect data drift, prediction drift, subgroup degradation.

再訓練與版本治理

符合 FDA PCCP（Ch04）的「變更協定」；每次更新留下 audit trail。

Align retraining with FDA PCCP's modification protocol (Ch04); keep audit trails for every update.

隱私技術

四把隱私武器

① 去識別化 (De-identification)

HIPAA Safe Harbor 18 種識別子刪除；DICOM 標籤遮罩；NER 自動找 PHI。最基本但不夠。

Remove 18 HIPAA identifiers; DICOM tag masking; NER-based PHI scrubbing. Necessary but insufficient.

② 差分隱私 (DP)

訓練時對梯度加雜訊，保證個人記錄對最終模型影響 < ε。Opacus（PyTorch）、TensorFlow Privacy。

Add noise to gradients during training so any record affects the final model by < ε. Opacus, TensorFlow Privacy.

③ 聯邦學習 (Federated Learning)

資料不出機構，只交換模型權重 / 梯度。Flower / NVIDIA FLARE / OpenFL；2024 NPJ Digital Medicine 報告 DP+FL 可在 1.57M WSI 上達中央訓練同等表現。

Data stays on-site; only weights/gradients move. Flower / NVIDIA FLARE / OpenFL; 2024 NPJ Digital Med shows DP+FL matches centralized training on 1.57M WSI.

④ 安全多方計算 / 同態加密

SMPC / HE 讓多方在不揭露原始資料的條件下聯合推論。計算成本仍高，2024 起逐漸落地於跨國臨床試驗。

SMPC / HE enable joint inference without revealing raw data. Compute costs remain high; entering multinational trials in 2024+.

💡
2024 聯邦學習 EHR 報告：多醫院糖尿病研究中，FL 把資料外洩風險降 40%，並把預測表現提升 15%。FL+DP 是 2024-2026 的「機構合作」黃金搭檔。 2024 federated EHR finding: in multi-hospital diabetes work, FL lowered breach risk 40% and improved prediction 15%. FL+DP is the 2024-2026 gold combo for institutional collaboration.

案例故事
2023-10 · 23andMe 700 萬用戶資料外洩

23andMe 外洩警世錄

2023 年 10 月，23andMe 公開 hacker 透過 credential stuffing 攻擊取得約700 萬用戶的部分基因 + 家族 + 族裔資料。其中 1 萬猶太裔用戶的資料被放上暗網販售；2024 起多個集體訴訟。

關鍵教訓：(1) 23andMe 沒強制 2FA；(2) 「DNA Relatives」社交功能允許 hacker 從少數帳號爆破關聯到數百萬人；(3) 公司初期推卸給用戶「自己沒設好密碼」，後來改口；(4) 2024 公司股價 -90%。

對 AI/MLOps 啟示：(1) 關聯資料風險被嚴重低估——你只能保護「自己的 PHI」，但家族成員的關聯無法 fully de-identify；(2) 2FA、rate limiting、隱私設計從一開始不是事後補救；(3) 事件回應透明度影響長期信任。

同期歐盟 GDPR 罰款 23andMe €350K；FTC 訴訟仍在進行。HHS 開始討論把基因資料也納入 HIPAA 直接管轄（目前只在「醫療機構處理」時受 HIPAA 管）。

In October 2023, 23andMe disclosed that hackers used credential stuffing to obtain partial genetic + family + ancestry data of ~7 million users. Data of 10,000 Jewish-ancestry users was sold on the dark web. Class actions followed in 2024.

Key lessons: (1) 23andMe didn't mandate 2FA; (2) the "DNA Relatives" social feature let hackers chain from a few accounts to millions; (3) the company initially blamed users for weak passwords, then reversed; (4) stock dropped 90% by 2024.

Implications for AI/MLOps: (1) relational data risk is severely under-rated — you can protect "your own PHI", but family-member linkages can't be fully de-identified; (2) 2FA, rate limiting, privacy-by-design from day one, not retroactively; (3) incident transparency shapes long-term trust.

EU fined 23andMe €350K under GDPR; FTC actions ongoing. HHS is considering bringing genetic data under direct HIPAA jurisdiction (currently only when "handled by a covered entity").

教訓：(1) 隱私設計從第 0 天；(2) 關聯資料無法完全去識別化；(3) 事件回應透明度 = 信任。
Lessons: (1) privacy by design from day 0; (2) relational data can't be fully de-identified; (3) incident transparency = trust.

Differential Privacy (DP)差分隱私
Dwork 2006；數學保證單筆資料對模型影響 ≤ ε。Opacus 是 PyTorch 實作。
Dwork 2006; math guarantee that a single record affects the model by ≤ ε. Opacus is the PyTorch implementation.
ε (Epsilon)隱私預算
DP 強度參數；ε ≤ 1 強隱私、ε > 10 名義隱私。生醫常 ε=4-8。
DP strength parameter; ε ≤ 1 strong, ε > 10 nominal. Biomedical: ε=4-8 commonly.
DP-SGD差分隱私 SGD
Abadi 2016；梯度裁剪 + Gaussian noise。Opacus 內建。
Abadi 2016; gradient clipping + Gaussian noise. Built into Opacus.
Federated Learning (FL)聯邦學習
McMahan 2017；資料不出機構，只交換 weights / gradients。Flower、FLARE、OpenFL 是主流框架。
McMahan 2017; data stays on-site, only weights/gradients exchanged. Flower, FLARE, OpenFL are mainstream.
FedAvg / FedProx聯邦聚合
FL 聚合演算法；FedProx 對 non-IID 資料更穩。
FL aggregation algorithms; FedProx handles non-IID data better.
SMPC安全多方計算
多方在不揭露原始資料下聯合計算；計算成本高。
Joint computation without revealing raw data; computationally expensive.
Homomorphic Encryption (HE)同態加密
加密狀態下做計算；Microsoft SEAL、HElib；2024-2026 仍主要用於 inference。
Compute on encrypted data; Microsoft SEAL, HElib; 2024-2026 mainly for inference.

Sweeney 2002；每個資料點與其他 k-1 個 indistinguishable；l-diversity, t-closeness 進一步加強。
Sweeney 2002; every record indistinguishable from k-1 others; l-diversity, t-closeness extend further.

想得更深

Q1. HIPAA Safe Harbor 18 項真的足夠嗎？Sweeney 1997 證明 87% 美國人可由「ZIP+生日+性別」重識別。對基因組（Gymrek 2013：STR → 姓氏）、影像（face reconstruction from MRI）更嚴重。行動：永遠搭配 k-anonymity / DP。
Q1. Is HIPAA Safe Harbor's 18 identifiers really enough?Sweeney 1997 showed 87% of US population is re-identifiable from ZIP+DOB+sex. Worse for genomes (Gymrek 2013: STR → surname), imaging (face reconstruction from MRI).Action: always combine with k-anonymity / DP.
Q2. 聯邦學習真的解決隱私嗎？FL 不送原始資料，但 gradient 仍可能洩漏。Membership inference attack、gradient inversion attack 都已展示。FL+DP+secure aggregation 三層才安全。參考：Geiping 2020 「inverting gradients」攻擊論文。
Q2. Does FL really solve privacy?FL doesn't send raw data, but gradients can still leak. Membership inference, gradient inversion attacks have been demonstrated. FL + DP + secure aggregation triple-layer is needed.Reference: Geiping 2020 "inverting gradients" attack paper.
Q3. ε 該設多少？沒有絕對標準。Apple iOS keyboard 用 ε ≤ 4；US Census 2020 用 ε=19.61；醫療研究常 ε=4-8。關鍵：報告 ε 與 δ + 預期 utility loss，讓 reviewer 判斷。經驗：先 ε=∞（無 DP）跑 baseline，再降到 ε=8、4、1 看 utility 曲線。
Q3. What ε should I use?No absolute standard. Apple iOS keyboard ε ≤ 4; US Census 2020 ε=19.61; biomedical research often ε=4-8. Key: report ε, δ, and expected utility loss so reviewers can judge.Practice: baseline at ε=∞ (no DP), then descend to ε=8, 4, 1 — observe utility curve.
Q4. FDA PCCP 對 MLOps 工程意義？PCCP 要求 (1) modification description；(2) modification protocol；(3) impact assessment——這些對應 MLOps 的 versioning + experiment tracking + monitoring。沒做好 MLOps 就無法通過 PCCP。行動：MLflow + DVC + Evidently 三件套是基本配備。
Q4. What does FDA PCCP mean for MLOps?PCCP requires (1) modification description; (2) modification protocol; (3) impact assessment — directly mapping to MLOps versioning + tracking + monitoring. Without solid MLOps, no PCCP.Action: MLflow + DVC + Evidently is the baseline triad.

HIPAA-equivalent + layer 2 suffices for most research; commercial deployments add 3, 4.
三選：(1) NVIDIA FLARE—企業級，醫療場景優化（COVID 多醫院研究用此）；(2) Flower—開源最廣泛，學術友善；(3) OpenFL—Intel 開源，HE 整合好；(4) FedML—商業 + 學術混合。台灣 NCHC + 台灣 AI Labs 已有聯邦學習 testbed。Three picks: (1) NVIDIA FLARE — enterprise-grade, medical-optimized (used in COVID multi-hospital studies); (2) Flower — most widely open-sourced, academic-friendly; (3) OpenFL — Intel open-source, good HE integration; (4) FedML — hybrid. Taiwan NCHC + Taiwan AI Labs have FL testbeds.
五項：(1) data drift—input 分布變化（KL、Wasserstein）；(2) prediction drift—輸出分布變化；(3) concept drift—X→Y 關係變化（hardest）；(4) 子族表現—Black / Asian / 女性等是否退化；(5) 系統指標—latency、error rate、cost。Evidently、Arize、WhyLogs 都可一站式。Five: (1) data drift — input distribution shifts (KL, Wasserstein); (2) prediction drift — output shifts; (3) concept drift — X→Y relationship changes (hardest); (4) subgroup performance — Black / Asian / female regression; (5) system metrics — latency, error rate, cost. Evidently, Arize, WhyLogs are one-stop tools.
