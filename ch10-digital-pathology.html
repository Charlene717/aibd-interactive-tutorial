挑戰

為什麼 WSI 是 AI 最硬的影像挑戰

一張全玻片影像（WSI）動輒 100,000 × 100,000 像素、約 1–10 GB。沒有任何 GPU 能整張塞進來。標準解法是切成 256² 或 512² 的 patches，用 multiple-instance learning (MIL) 在 patch-level 聚合到 slide-level 標籤。同時還要面對 H&E 染色差異、組織人工破損、跨醫院 scanner 差異。

2023-2024 起，病理 Foundation Models 改變遊戲規則：UNI、CONCH、Virchow、Prov-GigaPath 預訓在數十萬到百萬 WSI 上，提供強大 patch encoder，下游只要訓練 attention-MIL head 就能 SOTA。

A whole-slide image (WSI) is routinely 100,000 × 100,000 pixels, ~1–10 GB. No GPU can load it at once. Standard recipe: tile into 256² or 512² patches, use Multiple Instance Learning (MIL) to aggregate patch-level features into slide-level labels. Plus H&E stain variation, tissue artifacts, scanner-to-scanner differences across hospitals.

Since 2023–2024, pathology foundation models changed the game: UNI, CONCH, Virchow, Prov-GigaPath pretrain on hundreds of thousands to millions of WSIs, giving strong patch encoders so downstream only needs an attention-MIL head to reach SOTA.

想再深一點？Attention-MIL 的數學

給定一張 WSI 被切成 N 個 patches，每個 patch 經 encoder 得 h_i ∈ ℝ^d。Attention-MIL（Ilse 2018）學一個 attention vector a：

a_i = exp(w^T tanh(V h_i)) / Σⱼ exp(w^T tanh(V h_j))

然後 slide-level 表徵 z = Σ a_i h_i 用於分類。關鍵性質：(1) 每個 patch 貢獻可解釋（heatmap 直接疊回 WSI 給病理師看）；(2) 對 patch 數 N 不變；(3) permutation-invariant。

進階變體：

  • CLAM—兩個分支，正類 attention + 負類 attention，分別學「ROI vs 背景」
  • TransMIL—把 patches 視為序列丟 Transformer，建模 patch 間關係
  • DSMIL—dual-stream，加 max-pooling 補強
  • Prov-GigaPath—用 LongNet 把 1M+ patches 串成「slide-level Transformer」

A WSI splits into N patches; each gives h_i ∈ ℝ^d via the encoder. Attention-MIL (Ilse 2018) learns an attention vector a:

a_i = exp(w^T tanh(V h_i)) / Σⱼ exp(w^T tanh(V h_j))

The slide-level representation z = Σ a_i h_i feeds classification. Key properties: (1) per-patch contribution interpretable (heatmap overlays WSI for pathologists); (2) invariant to N; (3) permutation-invariant.

Variants:

  • CLAM — two branches, positive + negative attention; learns "ROI vs background"
  • TransMIL — patches as a sequence in a Transformer, modeling inter-patch relations
  • DSMIL — dual-stream + max-pooling reinforcement
  • Prov-GigaPath — LongNet strings 1M+ patches into a slide-level Transformer
案例故事
2024 · Mahmood Lab UNI 訓練

UNI 訓練的代價與報償

2024 年 Mahmood Lab 在 Nature Medicine 發表 UNI——病理史上第一個真正的 Foundation Model。訓練資料：>100K WSI、>100M patches，來自 Mass General Brigham 等 20+ 機構。架構：ViT-L + DINOv2（self-supervised contrastive）。

訓練成本：在 4 個 A100 上跑了 ~3 個月，總計 ~$80K USD GPU 費用。但 release 之後：14 個下游 benchmark 上幾乎全勝過 ResNet-50 + ImageNet baseline，平均 +9.3% balanced accuracy。

更重要：UNI 是非商業 open-weights。截至 2025-04，HuggingFace 下載量 50K+，被超過 200 篇下游論文引用。Mahmood Lab 隨後又釋出 CONCH（vision-language 版本，含 1.17M image-caption 對）。

2025 colon MSI benchmark 揭曉一個有趣結果：CONCH（用 ProtoNet adaptation）勝過 UNI、Virchow、Prov-GigaPath。原因：CONCH 多模態的 caption 對齊幫助下游 few-shot 更穩。

In 2024, Mahmood Lab published UNI in Nature Medicine — pathology's first true foundation model. Training data: >100K WSI, >100M patches from MGB and 20+ institutions. Architecture: ViT-L + DINOv2 (self-supervised contrastive).

Cost: ~3 months on 4× A100, totaling ~$80K USD GPU spend. But after release: it beat ResNet-50 + ImageNet baseline on 14/14 downstream benchmarks, +9.3% balanced accuracy on average.

Crucially: UNI is non-commercial open-weights. By 2025-04 it had 50K+ HuggingFace downloads and 200+ downstream citations. Mahmood Lab then released CONCH (vision-language variant, 1.17M image-caption pairs).

2025 colon MSI benchmark surprise: CONCH (with ProtoNet adaptation) beat UNI, Virchow, Prov-GigaPath. Why? CONCH's multimodal caption alignment helps few-shot downstream stability.

教訓：(1) 最大資料不等於最好下游；(2) 加 vision-language 對齊往往勝過純 vision 預訓；(3) Open-weights FM 是社群乘數效應的關鍵。
Lessons: (1) biggest data ≠ best downstream; (2) vision-language alignment often beats pure-vision pretraining; (3) open-weight FMs are the key multiplier for community impact.

想得更深

Q1. AI 病理會取代病理醫師嗎？UNI / CONCH 在 14 個 benchmark 勝 ResNet baseline，但臨床部署仍需病理師審核 attention heatmap。預期：未來 5 年是「AI 加速病理師」，10 年後可能改寫工作流。參考：FDA 2024 通過第一個 AI 病理 SaMD：Paige Prostate。
Q1. Will AI replace pathologists?UNI/CONCH beat ResNet on 14 benchmarks, but clinical deployment still needs pathologist review of attention heatmaps. Expectation: next 5 years = "AI accelerates pathologists"; 10 years may rewrite workflow.Reference: FDA 2024 cleared the first AI pathology SaMD — Paige Prostate.
Q2. 商業 (Virchow) vs 開源 (UNI / CONCH)：誰會贏？商業有資源也有資料 (Paige 1.5M WSI)；開源有社群動量。歷史上 NLP 開源（BERT、Llama）勝過閉源，病理可能類似。行動：學界優先 UNI/CONCH，產業可比較成本/支援。
Q2. Commercial (Virchow) vs open (UNI/CONCH) — who wins?Commercial has resources + data (Paige 1.5M WSI); open has community momentum. Historically NLP open (BERT, Llama) beat closed; pathology may follow.Action: academia → UNI/CONCH; industry → compare cost/support.
Q3. WSI 染色標準化做 vs 不做？Macenko 等方法在同機構內收益小（<1pp），但跨機構收益可達 5-10pp。FM 訓練資料若已多元，染色標準化重要性下降。實驗：先測量你的 cross-institution 表現差異。
Q3. Stain normalize or not?Macenko etc. gain little (<1pp) within institution; cross-institution gains 5-10pp. If FM training data is already diverse, importance decreases.Experiment: measure your cross-institution gap first.
Q4. 亞洲組織學樣本能用歐美訓練的 FM 嗎？多數 FM 訓練資料以歐美為主。亞洲族群在某些癌型（如 HCC、ESCC、gastric cancer）有不同形態學特徵。建議：跨亞洲機構合作 fine-tune 一個 Asian-pathology FM 是當前機會。
Q4. Can Western-trained FMs serve Asian histology samples?Most FMs are Western-skewed. Asian populations show distinct morphology in some cancers (HCC, ESCC, gastric). Suggestion: collaborative pan-Asian fine-tuning of an Asian-pathology FM is an open opportunity.

三條路：(1) MIL 弱監督—只需 slide-level label，不需 pixel-level；(2) Active learning—讓 AI 挑「最不確定」的 slide 給病理師看；(3) FM zero/few-shot—CONCH text prompt 可在 5-10 examples 達到 0.85+ accuracy。Mass General Brigham 用這套方法把 1000-slide 任務從 6 個月縮到 2 週。Three paths: (1) weakly supervised MIL — only slide-level labels, not pixel-level; (2) active learning — AI picks the "most uncertain" slides for pathologists; (3) FM zero/few-shot — CONCH text prompts reach 0.85+ accuracy with 5-10 examples.
