多模態 Foundation Models

影像 + 文字 + 報告 + EHR + 訊號——醫療 AI 進入「同時看與說」的時代。

Image + text + report + EHR + signal — medical AI in the era of "seeing & speaking together."

新手友善 中階 進階補充

這一章你會學到什麼？

From CLIP newcomers to deployed medical-AI engineers, this is the multimodal FM panorama:

  • Differentiate Med-Gemini, BiomedCLIP, LLaVA-Med, Path-Foundation, RoentGen

  • Steer clear of "more modalities is better" and "text-to-X-ray has no ethics issue" myths
為什麼多模態

醫療診斷天生是多模態的

胸 X 光 + 病史 + 抽血 + 心電圖 + 生命徵象——沒有醫生只看單一資料下診斷。但 2020 年前的醫療 AI 多半「單模態」，每個模型只專一資料。多模態 Foundation Model 透過共同的嵌入空間，把不同模態接到同一個語意層，從而：

  • 用影像 + 報告做零樣本分類（CLIP-style）。
  • Zero-shot classification from image + report (CLIP-style).
  • 由影像直接生成放射報告草稿。
  • Generate radiology report drafts directly from images.
  • 跨模態檢索：以症狀文字找相似影像、以影像找相似病歷。
  • Cross-modal retrieval: text symptoms → similar images, image → similar charts.
  • VQA（Visual Question Answering）：醫生口述問題，AI 對影像作答。
  • VQA: clinician asks in natural language, AI answers about the image.

架構模式

三種主流結合方式

① Late fusion (CLIP-style)

各模態各自 encoder → 同維度向量 → cosine 對齊。優點：簡單、易加新模態；缺點：早期細節對齊弱。

Per-modal encoders → same-dim vectors → cosine alignment. Pros: simple, easy to add modalities. Cons: weak early alignment.

② Cross-attention

用 cross-attention 把一模態的 query 注入另一模態（如 LLaVA-Med 把影像 token 注入 LLM）。

Cross-attention queries one modality from another (e.g., LLaVA-Med injects image tokens into the LLM).

③ Token-mixed

把所有模態 token 化後丟同一 Transformer。Med-Gemini、ESM-3 用這個範式。

Tokenize all modalities into one Transformer. Used by Med-Gemini, ESM-3.

實作

BiomedCLIP 跨模零樣本

from open_clip import create_model_from_pretrained, get_tokenizer
from PIL import Image
import torch
m, prep = create_model_from_pretrained("hf-hub:microsoft/BiomedCLIP-PubMedBERT_256-vit_base_patch16_224")
tok = get_tokenizer("hf-hub:microsoft/BiomedCLIP-PubMedBERT_256-vit_base_patch16_224")
img = prep(Image.open("chest_xray.png")).unsqueeze(0)
labels = ["pneumonia", "normal", "effusion", "pneumothorax"]
texts = tok(["this is a chest x-ray showing "+l for l in labels])
with torch.no_grad():
    img_feat = m.encode_image(img); txt_feat = m.encode_text(texts)
    img_feat = img_feat / img_feat.norm(dim=-1, keepdim=True)
    txt_feat = txt_feat / txt_feat.norm(dim=-1, keepdim=True)
    sim = (100.0 * img_feat @ txt_feat.T).softmax(-1)
for l, p in zip(labels, sim[0]): print(l, round(p.item(),3))
library(reticulate); library(magick)
open_clip <- import("open_clip"); torch <- import("torch")
ck <- "hf-hub:microsoft/BiomedCLIP-PubMedBERT_256-vit_base_patch16_224"
out <- open_clip$create_model_from_pretrained(ck)
m   <- out[[1]]; prep <- out[[2]]
tok <- open_clip$get_tokenizer(ck)
img <- prep(magick::image_read("chest_xray.png"))$unsqueeze(0L)
labels <- c("pneumonia","normal","effusion","pneumothorax")
texts  <- tok(paste("this is a chest x-ray showing", labels))
真正的多模態 AI 不是「把模態拼起來」，是「學會在共同潛在空間裡，讓影像和文字成為彼此的翻譯」。 — Radford et al. 2021 CLIP paper
Real multimodal AI isn't "stitching modalities" — it's "making image and text translate each other in a shared latent space". — Radford et al. 2021 CLIP paper

想再深一點？InfoNCE loss

CLIP（Radford 2021）的核心訓練目標：給 N 個 (image, text) 配對，學一個圖像 encoder f_I 和文字 encoder f_T，使配對的 cosine similarity 高、未配對的低。

L_InfoNCE = − (1/N) Σ_i log [ exp(sim(f_I(xᵢ), f_T(tᵢ))/τ) / Σⱼ exp(sim(f_I(xᵢ), f_T(tⱼ))/τ) ]

對稱地，也訓練 text→image 方向，最終 loss = (L_i2t + L_t2i)/2。τ 是 temperature。N=2 億對 (LAION-400M、LAION-2B) 預訓 → 通用視覺語言對齊。

BiomedCLIP（MS 2023）把 image encoder 換 ViT-B、text encoder 換 PubMedBERT，在 PubMed 圖文對上 fine-tune；下游 zero-shot 醫學影像分類勝過原始 CLIP +10pp。

CLIP's (Radford 2021) core: given N (image, text) pairs, learn image encoder f_I and text encoder f_T such that paired cosine similarity is high, unpaired low.

L_InfoNCE = − (1/N) Σ_i log [ exp(sim(f_I(xᵢ), f_T(tᵢ))/τ) / Σⱼ exp(sim(f_I(xᵢ), f_T(tⱼ))/τ) ]

Symmetric text→image training; final loss = (L_i2t + L_t2i)/2. τ is temperature. Pretrained on N=200M pairs (LAION-400M, LAION-2B) → general vision-language alignment.

BiomedCLIP (MS 2023) swaps image encoder for ViT-B and text encoder for PubMedBERT, fine-tunes on PubMed image-text pairs; downstream zero-shot medical-imaging classification beats vanilla CLIP +10pp.

案例故事
2024 · Med-Gemini 在 NEJM Image Challenge

Med-Gemini 在 NEJM Image Challenge

NEJM Image Challenge 每週發布一個臨床影像 + 問題，全球醫師線上作答。2024 Google 研究團隊用 Med-Gemini 跑了過去 5 年 240 個 case，整體答對率 69%——比 NEJM 讀者平均（44%）高 25pp，比住院醫師（55%）高 14pp。

更有趣的是 case-level 分析：(1) 純放射科影像 Med-Gemini 強（皮膚科、眼科、影像）；(2) 需病史推理表現 OK；(3) 需 EHR + 影像 + 實驗室整合的案例，Med-Gemini 與住院醫師打平。

關鍵：Med-Gemini 是 token-mixed multimodal——影像、文字、表格、ECG 都 tokenize 進同一個 Transformer，跟 LLaVA 的 cross-attention 不同。Google 認為這是「模態統一」的未來方向。

但也有陷阱：Med-Gemini 對「看起來像但實則不同」的影像（如 Kaposi vs bacillary angiomatosis）仍會誤判。發表的研究謹慎標明：「Med-Gemini 是 輔助診斷，不是取代臨床推理」。

NEJM Image Challenge releases a clinical image + question weekly; global doctors answer online. In 2024 Google ran Med-Gemini on the past 5 years' 240 cases — overall accuracy 69%, +25pp over NEJM readers (44%), +14pp over residents (55%).

Case-level analysis: (1) pure radiology imaging Med-Gemini strong (derm, ophth, imaging); (2) history-reasoning OK; (3) EHR + imaging + lab integration, Med-Gemini ties with residents.

Key: Med-Gemini is token-mixed multimodal — images, text, tables, ECGs all tokenize into one Transformer, unlike LLaVA's cross-attention. Google sees this as the future of "unified modality".

Pitfalls remain: Med-Gemini misjudges visually similar but distinct conditions (e.g., Kaposi vs bacillary angiomatosis). The paper carefully marks: "Med-Gemini is diagnostic assistance, not a replacement for clinical reasoning".

教訓：多模態 AI 在影像 + 文字勝過住院醫師，但「複雜臨床推理」仍需人；定位是「副駕」不是「主駕」。
Lesson: multimodal AI beats residents on image + text, but "complex clinical reasoning" still needs humans; the role is "copilot" not "pilot".

想得更深

Q1. RoentGen 從文字生成 X 光的倫理？合成資料增廣訓練集 OK，但若用來「fake 病人影像」訓練診斷 AI 會災難——學到的是 RoentGen 的偏差而非真實病理。FDA 2025 草案明令合成資料須附 fidelity 評估。辯論：罕病合成資料能否用於 pivotal study？
Q1. Ethics of RoentGen text-to-X-ray?Synthetic augmentation OK; but training diagnostic AI on "fake patient images" is disastrous — it learns RoentGen's biases, not real pathology. FDA 2025 draft requires fidelity assessment for synthetic data.Debate: synthetic rare-disease data for pivotal studies?
Q2. 影像-報告自動生成可信嗎？RadFM, MAIRA-2 等可生成放射報告草稿。問題：(1) hallucination（無中生有）；(2) 漏報（incidental findings）；(3) 法律責任。2024-2025 部署都採「AI 起草 + 放射科簽署」混合模式。參考：Mayo Clinic 2024 AI report drafting 內部評估。
Q2. Trust AI-generated radiology reports?RadFM, MAIRA-2 can draft. Problems: (1) hallucination; (2) missed incidental findings; (3) legal liability. 2024-2025 deployments use "AI drafts + radiologist signs" hybrid.Reference: Mayo Clinic 2024 internal AI report drafting evaluation.
Q3. Token-mixed (Med-Gemini) vs Cross-attention (LLaVA)：誰會贏？Google 押注 token-mixed；Meta / Stanford 多 cross-attention。實證：在 large-scale data 上 token-mixed 略勝（Med-Gemini）；data-poor scenarios cross-attention 更高效率。假設：2026+ 主流會收斂到 token-mixed（隨資料規模成長）。
Q3. Token-mixed (Med-Gemini) vs Cross-attention (LLaVA): who wins?Google bets on token-mixed; Meta/Stanford lean cross-attention. Empirically: token-mixed slightly wins on large data (Med-Gemini); cross-attention more efficient with limited data.Hypothesis: 2026+ mainstream converges to token-mixed as data scales.
Q4. 多模態 hallucination 怎麼緩解？三條路：(1) RAG grounding（醫療知識庫對齊）；(2) chain-of-thought + 引用追溯；(3) uncertainty quantification。MMHal-V benchmark 顯示 GPT-4V hallucination 率 ~12%，Med-Gemini ~7%——仍很高。行動：臨床部署永遠 human-in-the-loop。
Q4. Mitigating multimodal hallucination?Three paths: (1) RAG grounding (medical KB alignment); (2) chain-of-thought + citation tracing; (3) uncertainty quantification. MMHal-V shows GPT-4V hallucination ~12%, Med-Gemini ~7% — still high.Action: clinical deployment always human-in-the-loop.
