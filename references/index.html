補充說明 · Notes

Ch02 · UK Biobank 的歐洲族裔代表性偏差

教學中以 UK Biobank（~500K 受試者）為「大型族群資料」代表，但其 ≥94% 為自報歐洲血統，且加入者比英國一般族群更健康、社經地位更高（「healthy volunteer」效應）。Fry et al. 2017 (Am J Epidemiol, DOI:10.1093/aje/kwx246) 已警示外部效度限制。將以 UKB 訓練的 PRS / 風險模型直接套用於非歐洲族群，效能會明顯下降（Martin et al. 2019, Nat Genet, DOI:10.1038/s41588-019-0379-x）。建議搭配 All of Us、Biobank Japan、TOPMed、H3Africa 等更具多樣性的資料。

補充說明 · Notes

Ch19 · 聯邦學習並非萬靈隱私保護

教學常把 federated learning（FL）描述為「資料不出院區即可訓練模型」的隱私解方，但 共享梯度本身會洩漏訓練資料。Zhu et al. 2019 NeurIPS《Deep Leakage from Gradients (DLG)》（arXiv:1906.08935）證明可從單一 mini-batch 的梯度反推原始影像與標籤；Geiping et al. 2020 NeurIPS《Inverting Gradients》（arXiv:2003.14053）將攻擊擴展到 ImageNet 解析度、CNN 與大 batch。Boenisch et al. 2023《When the Curious Abandon Honesty》進一步示範 malicious server 可主動操控初始權重以放大洩漏。因此 FL 必須搭配 differential privacy（DP-SGD）、secure aggregation、homomorphic encryption 才有實質保護；只說「資料不離開機構」是不夠的。

Federated learning (FL) is often presented as "train without data leaving the hospital," but shared gradients themselves leak training data. Zhu et al. 2019 NeurIPS, Deep Leakage from Gradients (arXiv:1906.08935) reconstruct images and labels from a single mini-batch gradient; Geiping et al. 2020 NeurIPS, Inverting Gradients (arXiv:2003.14053) extend the attack to ImageNet resolution, CNNs and large batches. Boenisch et al. 2023 (When the Curious Abandon Honesty) show a malicious server can manipulate initial weights to amplify leakage. FL therefore needs DP-SGD, secure aggregation, or homomorphic encryption — "data stays on-prem" alone is insufficient.

補充說明 · Notes

Ch04 / Ch19 · 合成資料的效用-隱私 trade-off

合成 EHR / 影像常被視為「無 PHI 的開放資料」捷徑，但 Stadler, Oprisanu & Troncoso 2022 USENIX Security《Synthetic Data — Anonymisation Groundhog Day》（arXiv:2011.07018）證明：(1) 不附隱私保證的合成資料對 membership inference 與 attribute disclosure 仍有可量化洩漏；(2) 強到能阻擋攻擊的隱私參數會把資料的實用性（downstream model AUC）拉到接近隨機。Annamalai & De Cristofaro 2024《Linear-Time Membership Inference Attacks on Synthetic Data》進一步降低攻擊門檻。教學若推薦合成資料應同時要求：明確的隱私機制（如 DP-GAN、PATE-GAN、DP-Diffusion）、效用評估與攻擊壓力測試，並避免把「合成 = 安全」當作預設。

Synthetic EHR / imaging is often pitched as the "PHI-free open-data shortcut," but Stadler, Oprisanu & Troncoso 2022 USENIX Security, Synthetic Data — Anonymisation Groundhog Day (arXiv:2011.07018) show (i) synthetic data without privacy guarantees still leaks measurably to membership-inference and attribute-disclosure attacks, and (ii) privacy parameters strong enough to defeat attacks crush downstream utility (AUC) toward random. Annamalai & De Cristofaro 2024 further lower the attack bar. Require an explicit mechanism (DP-GAN, PATE-GAN, DP-Diffusion), utility evaluation, and adversarial stress tests — do not treat "synthetic = safe."

補充說明 · Notes

Ch15 / Ch20 · Foundation Model 部署的能耗與經濟成本

推銷醫療 FM（Med-PaLM 2、Med-Gemini、UNI、Prov-GigaPath）時，教學若只談效能，會忽略 訓練與推論的碳排與成本不對等。Strubell et al. 2019 ACL《Energy and Policy Considerations for Deep Learning in NLP》（P19-1355）估計訓練單一大型 NLP 模型 CO₂ 排放可與多輛汽車整個生命週期相當；Schwartz et al. 2020 Comm. ACM《Green AI》（DOI:10.1145/3381831）倡議把 efficiency（FLOPs、$/query、kWh）列為與 accuracy 同等重要的評估指標。Patterson et al. 2021、Luccioni et al. 2023 (BLOOM) 揭露推論累積能耗常超過訓練。低資源國家、小型診所無法負擔 H100 cluster + 24/7 推論，會放大 health equity gap，建議在課程中與 distillation、quantization（GPTQ、AWQ）、edge inference 一併討論。

When advocating medical FMs (Med-PaLM 2, Med-Gemini, UNI, Prov-GigaPath), the tutorial must surface uneven energy and economic costs. Strubell et al. 2019 ACL, Energy and Policy Considerations for Deep Learning in NLP (P19-1355) estimate training a single large NLP model can emit CO₂ comparable to several cars over their lifetime; Schwartz et al. 2020 Comm. ACM, Green AI (DOI:10.1145/3381831) urges efficiency (FLOPs, $/query, kWh) as a first-class metric. Patterson 2021 and Luccioni 2023 (BLOOM) show inference often dominates lifetime energy. Small clinics and LMICs cannot afford H100 clusters with 24/7 inference, which widens the health-equity gap — teach distillation, quantization (GPTQ, AWQ) and edge inference alongside.

補充說明 · Notes

Ch04 · Obermeyer 2019 — 看不見的 proxy bias

教學若僅以「資料中沒有 race 欄位」作為演算法公平的論證是不安全的。Obermeyer et al. 2019 Science（DOI:10.1126/science.aax2342）解構一個影響數千萬美國人的 health-risk algorithm：模型完全沒有用 race 變數，但以「歷年醫療支出」作為「健康風險」的 proxy。由於黑人病人因系統性歧視在相同病情下花費較少醫療資源，演算法把同樣生病的黑人病人風險評估得明顯較低，少了一半的高風險者被轉介到 care management。修正 target（從 cost 改為 illness）可使種族差距下降 84%。教訓：fairness 必須檢查 label / target / proxy 的選擇本身，而不是只看模型輸入。延伸閱讀：Mitchell et al. 2019《Model Cards》、Gebru et al. 2021《Datasheets for Datasets》。

Removing the race column does not make an algorithm fair. Obermeyer et al. 2019 Science (DOI:10.1126/science.aax2342) audited a health-risk algorithm covering tens of millions of US patients: it never used race but used historical medical spending as a proxy for health need. Because Black patients spend less for the same illness due to structural barriers, the algorithm rated equally sick Black patients as lower-risk and halved their referral rate to care management. Re-targeting on illness instead of cost reduced racial disparity by 84%. Lesson: fairness must interrogate label / target / proxy choice, not just inputs. See also Mitchell et al. 2019 (Model Cards) and Gebru et al. 2021 (Datasheets for Datasets).

補充說明 · Notes

Ch19 · DP-SGD 的 ε 該設多少？

教學常說「我們用 differential privacy 訓練」便止步，未告訴讀者 ε 與 δ 該設多少、會如何影響效能。Abadi et al. 2016 CCS《Deep Learning with Differential Privacy》（DOI:10.1145/2976749.2978318）提出 DP-SGD 與 moments accountant，建議 ε ≈ 1–8、δ < 1/n（n 為訓練樣本數）為合理區間。實務上：MNIST 等小資料即使 ε=2 也很穩，但生醫高維資料（基因組、WSI patch）常需 ε=8 以上才能維持效用，而此時隱私保證已很弱（Apple 過去用 ε≈14 被批評；US Census 2020 用 ε=19.61 的 zCDP 轉換）。Jagielski et al. 2020、Nasr et al. 2021 提供 audit attack 來「驗證 ε」。教學應同時揭露 (ε, δ)、clip norm、噪聲乘子 σ、accountant 與 baseline utility 才有意義。

Saying "we trained with DP" without disclosing (ε, δ) is meaningless. Abadi et al. 2016 CCS, Deep Learning with Differential Privacy (DOI:10.1145/2976749.2978318) introduced DP-SGD and the moments accountant; ε ≈ 1–8 and δ < 1/n is a reasonable range, but biomedical high-dim data (genomes, WSI patches) often needs ε ≥ 8 to retain utility, at which point the formal guarantee is weak (Apple historically used ε ≈ 14; US Census 2020 used ε = 19.61 under zCDP). Jagielski 2020 and Nasr 2021 provide auditing attacks. A meaningful DP claim discloses (ε, δ), clip norm, noise multiplier σ, accountant, and baseline utility.
