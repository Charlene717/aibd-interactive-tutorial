BLOCK A · CHAPTER 01 / 20

AI × 生醫的時代背景

從序列、結構到語言模型——理解這一波 AI 風暴為什麼正在重寫生醫研究的版圖。

From sequence to structure to language models — why this AI wave is rewriting the biomedical research playbook.

為什麼是現在

生醫研究的「ImageNet 時刻」已經到了

2012 年 AlexNet 用一張 GPU 把 ImageNet 錯誤率砍半，催生了電腦視覺的深度學習革命。十年後，AlphaFold 2 (2021) 用相同的「大模型 + 大數據」配方，把蛋白質結構預測拉到接近實驗精度——這是生醫的 ImageNet 時刻。

到 2024-2026 年，這個典範持續放大：AlphaFold 3 在單一模型中預測蛋白質、核酸、配體、離子的聯合結構；scGPT、Geneformer、UCE 把 Transformer 應用在數千萬個單細胞上，做到零樣本細胞註釋；Med-Gemini 在 USMLE MedQA 拿到 91.1% 的新 SOTA；AlphaMissense 把人類所有 7100 萬個錯義變異標上致病性機率。

這些工作的共通點是：不是更聰明的算法，而是更大的模型 × 更多的資料 × 更便宜的算力。生醫資料正在進入 zettabyte 等級，問題從「資料不夠」轉成「如何把對的模型架構套到對的資料上」。

In 2012, AlexNet halved ImageNet error rates using a single GPU and ignited the deep learning revolution in vision. A decade later, AlphaFold 2 (2021) applied the same "big model + big data" recipe to push protein structure prediction to near-experimental accuracy — biomedicine's ImageNet moment.

By 2024–2026, the paradigm has compounded: AlphaFold 3 jointly predicts structures of proteins, nucleic acids, ligands and ions in one model; scGPT, Geneformer, UCE apply Transformers to tens of millions of single cells for zero-shot annotation; Med-Gemini sets a new SOTA at 91.1% on MedQA (USMLE); AlphaMissense labels pathogenicity probabilities for all 71M human missense variants.

Their shared lesson: not smarter algorithms but bigger models × more data × cheaper compute. Biomedical data is hitting zettabyte scale; the bottleneck has shifted from "not enough data" to "matching the right architecture to the right data."

💡
核心觀察：2024 年起，幾乎每個生醫子領域都已經出現自己的 Foundation Model——基因體（Caduceus）、單細胞（scGPT）、病理（UNI / CONCH / Virchow）、影像（MedSAM）、臨床（Med-Gemini）、蛋白質（ESM-3 / AlphaFold 3）。本教程要做的，就是帶你看清這張地圖。 Key observation: Since 2024, almost every biomedical subfield has its own foundation model — genomics (Caduceus), single-cell (scGPT), pathology (UNI / CONCH / Virchow), imaging (MedSAM), clinical (Med-Gemini), proteins (ESM-3 / AlphaFold 3). This tutorial maps that landscape for you.

BERT (2018) & BioBERT (2019)

語言模型架構成熟。BioBERT 把 BERT 預訓練在 PubMed 與 PMC，開啟臨床/生醫 NLP 新時代。

Language-model architecture matures. BioBERT pretrains BERT on PubMed/PMC, launching the modern era of biomedical NLP.

單細胞分析從「PCA + Louvain」進入深度學習；變分自編碼器成為整合主力。

AlphaFold 2 (2021)

CASP14 接近實驗精度。Nature 全文 + 開源權重 + EBI 200M 結構庫，徹底改寫結構生物學。

CASP14 near-experimental accuracy. Nature paper + open weights + EBI 200M structure database rewrites structural biology.

ESMFold · ChatGPT (2022)

Meta ESM-2 用語言模型解 3D 結構；ChatGPT 把 LLM 推向大眾。生醫從業者開始重新評估「會講話的 AI」。

Meta's ESM-2 solves 3D structure via language modeling; ChatGPT brings LLMs to the masses. Biomedical practitioners reassess "AI that talks."

AlphaMissense · Med-PaLM 2 (2023)

Science 封面：AlphaMissense 覆蓋全人類錯義變異。Med-PaLM 2 在 MedQA 達 86.5%。

Science cover: AlphaMissense covers all human missense variants. Med-PaLM 2 hits 86.5% on MedQA.

AlphaFold 3 · scGPT · Med-Gemini (2024)

AF3 統一蛋白-核酸-配體預測；scGPT 33M 細胞預訓練；Med-Gemini 91.1% MedQA；FDA 12 月發布 PCCP 終版指引；EU AI Act 8 月生效。

AF3 unifies protein-NA-ligand prediction; scGPT pretrained on 33M cells; Med-Gemini 91.1% MedQA; FDA's final PCCP guidance (Dec 3); EU AI Act in force (Aug 1).

CZI rBio · Virtual Cell (2025)

Chan Zuckerberg Initiative 推 rBio，第一個用「虛擬細胞模擬」訓練的推理模型；Biohub 投入 $500M Virtual Biology Initiative。

CZI launches rBio — the first reasoning model trained on virtual-cell simulations; Biohub commits $500M to the Virtual Biology Initiative.

RFdiffusion 3 · Foundation Models 普及 (2026)

RFdiffusion3 開源、原子級設計、速度提升 10×。每個生醫子領域至少有一個 open-weights Foundation Model。

RFdiffusion3 open-sourced, atom-level design, 10× faster. Every biomedical subfield has at least one open-weights foundation model.

四股力量

驅動這場革命的四股力量

📊

① 資料規模

UK Biobank 50 萬人全基因組 + 影像 + EHR；Human Cell Atlas 已標註 5000 萬細胞；TCGA / GTEx / ENCODE 持續擴增。資料是燃料，而生醫的燃料庫從未這麼滿過。

UK Biobank: 500K whole genomes + imaging + EHR; HCA: 50M annotated cells; TCGA/GTEx/ENCODE continue to expand. Data is fuel — biomedicine has never been this well-fueled.

⚡

② 算力與架構

H100/B200 GPU、Transformer + 注意力機制、混合專家 (MoE)、Diffusion Models、Mamba/SSM。生醫從「線性模型 + 統計檢定」進入「上百億參數模型 + 預訓練 + 微調」的時代。

H100/B200 GPUs, Transformer + attention, Mixture-of-Experts, diffusion models, Mamba/SSM. Biomedicine has moved from "linear models + statistical tests" to "tens of billions of parameters + pretrain + fine-tune."

🧠

③ 開源權重與工具

Hugging Face、PyTorch、JAX、scverse、MONAI、Bionemo。Foundation Models 多半 open-weights（ESM-3、Geneformer、scGPT、UNI），讓中小實驗室也能站在巨人肩上微調。

Hugging Face, PyTorch, JAX, scverse, MONAI, BioNeMo. Most foundation models are open-weight (ESM-3, Geneformer, scGPT, UNI), letting small labs stand on giants' shoulders and fine-tune.

🏛️

④ 監管與基礎建設

FDA PCCP（2024/12）、EU AI Act（2024/1689）、ICH E14、TRIPOD-AI、NIH STRIDES 雲端。第一次有清楚的「AI 醫材」上市路徑與更新機制。

FDA PCCP (Dec 2024), EU AI Act (2024/1689), ICH E14, TRIPOD-AI, NIH STRIDES cloud. For the first time, there is a clear path-to-market and update mechanism for AI medical devices.

第一段程式碼

Hello, AI × Bio

讓我們用最少的程式碼，跑一個跨領域的範例：用 ESM-2 把一段蛋白質序列轉成嵌入向量，並比較兩條序列的相似度。這正是現代生醫 AI 的核心動作：序列 → 向量 → 下游任務。

Let's run the minimal cross-field example: use ESM-2 to embed protein sequences and compare two of them. This is the modern AI-for-bio motif: sequence → vector → downstream task.

# pip install fair-esm torch
import torch, esm
model, alphabet = esm.pretrained.esm2_t6_8M_UR50D()
batch_converter = alphabet.get_batch_converter()
seqs = [("p1", "MKTAYIAKQRQISFVKSHFSRQ"),
        ("p2", "MKTAYIAKQRQISFVKSHFSRX")]
_, _, tokens = batch_converter(seqs)
with torch.no_grad():
    out = model(tokens, repr_layers=[6])
emb = out["representations"][6].mean(1)
sim = torch.cosine_similarity(emb[0], emb[1], dim=0)
print(f"cosine = {sim.item():.4f}")
# reticulate + Python ESM —  R 直接呼叫 Python 模型
library(reticulate)
esm  <- import("esm")
torch <- import("torch")
pre  <- esm$pretrained$esm2_t6_8M_UR50D()
model <- pre[[1]]; alpha <- pre[[2]]
bc <- alpha$get_batch_converter()
seqs <- list(tuple("p1","MKTAYIAKQRQISFVKSHFSRQ"),
              tuple("p2","MKTAYIAKQRQISFVKSHFSRX"))
res <- bc(seqs)
out <- model(res[[3]], repr_layers=list(6L))
emb <- out$representations[[6L]]$mean(1L)
cat("cosine =", torch$cosine_similarity(emb[1], emb[2], dim=0L)$item())

本章小測 (3 題)

1. 下列哪一項不是促成 2021 年以後 AI × 生醫爆發的關鍵？

1. Which of the following is NOT a key driver behind the AI × Biomedicine boom since 2021?

A. UK Biobank / HCA 等大規模公開資料A. Large public datasets (UK Biobank, HCA)
B. Transformer 架構與 GPU 算力B. Transformer architectures and GPU compute
C. 大幅放寬的醫療資料隱私法規C. Drastically relaxed medical data privacy regulations
D. 開源權重（ESM、Geneformer、scGPT）的釋出D. Open-weights releases (ESM, Geneformer, scGPT)
正解：C。事實上 GDPR / HIPAA / EU AI Act 都更嚴格，AI 必須學會在強隱私約束下運作（聯邦學習、差分隱私）。Answer: C. GDPR/HIPAA/EU AI Act became stricter; AI had to learn to work under tight privacy constraints (federated learning, differential privacy).

2. AlphaFold 3 相對於 AlphaFold 2 最關鍵的新能力是？

2. The most critical new capability of AlphaFold 3 over AlphaFold 2 is?

A. 把模型參數縮小到原本的 1/10A. Reducing parameters to 1/10 of AF2
B. 在單一模型中聯合預測蛋白質-核酸-配體-離子的結構B. Joint prediction of protein–nucleic acid–ligand–ion structures in one model
C. 開放完整商業使用C. Fully open commercial use
D. 取代質譜實驗D. Replacing mass spectrometry
正解：B。AF3 採用擴散式架構，Nature 630:493–500 (2024)，把藥物設計關鍵的「配體結合姿態」整進主流程。Answer: B. AF3's diffusion architecture (Nature 630:493–500, 2024) brings ligand-binding pose — central to drug design — into the main pipeline.

3. 「Foundation Model」相較於傳統監督式深度學習最大的差別是？

3. The biggest difference between a "Foundation Model" and traditional supervised deep learning is?

A. 完全不需要 GPUA. It needs no GPU
B. 只在小資料上訓練B. It's trained only on small datasets
C. 先在大量無標註資料上自監督預訓練，再以少量標註資料微調或零樣本應用C. Self-supervised pretraining on massive unlabeled data, then few-shot / zero-shot downstream
D. 必須使用強化學習D. Must use reinforcement learning
正解：C。這正是 scGPT、ESM-3、UNI、Med-Gemini 共通的訓練範式：大規模自監督預訓練 ⇒ 下游任務通用。Answer: C. This is the shared paradigm of scGPT, ESM-3, UNI, Med-Gemini: massive self-supervised pretraining ⇒ general-purpose downstream.
