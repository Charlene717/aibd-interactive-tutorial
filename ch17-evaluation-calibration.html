新手友善 中階 進階補充

這一章你會學到什麼？

你也許聽過「我們的模型 AUC 有 0.95」這種句子。但你知道嗎？2021 年 Epic Sepsis Model 號稱 AUC 0.76 上線後，被 JAMA 研究發現臨床上實際 PPV 只有 7%——也就是說，AI 提醒 100 次只有 7 次真的對。這一章將告訴你：

  • 為什麼「AUROC 高」≠「臨床有用」，要追加 AUPRC、Brier、校準、Decision Curve、子族 AUC。
  • 怎麼修正校準錯誤：Platt scaling vs Isotonic vs Temperature scaling 怎麼選。
  • TRIPOD-AI 2024 / FDA PCCP / EU AI Act 對「模型卡」「外部驗證」的硬性要求。
  • 子族公平性檢查：整體 AUC 0.9，但對女性 / 黑人 / 罕病可能只有 0.6。

不需要先會深度學習；只要知道「機率」「混淆矩陣」就能讀懂全章。

You may have heard "our model hits AUC 0.95!" But did you know: in 2021, the Epic Sepsis Model shipped at AUC 0.76 — yet a JAMA study found its real-world PPV was just 7%. That means 93 out of 100 AI alerts were false. This chapter teaches:

  • Why "high AUROC" ≠ "clinically useful" — you also need AUPRC, Brier, calibration, decision curve, subgroup AUC.
  • How to fix miscalibration: Platt scaling vs Isotonic vs Temperature scaling — when to pick which.
  • What TRIPOD-AI 2024 / FDA PCCP / EU AI Act require for model cards & external validation.
  • Subgroup fairness: overall AUC 0.9, but possibly 0.6 for women / Black patients / rare disease.

No deep-learning background needed; if you know "probability" and "confusion matrix" you can follow the whole chapter.

觀念階梯

「Calibration」三層說明

🌱 新手版本

如果模型對 100 個病人都說「70% 機率癌症」，那真正有癌症的應該大約是 70 人。如果只有 30 人真的有，那這個模型「過度自信」、校準錯誤。

If the model says "70% chance of cancer" for 100 patients, about 70 should really have cancer. If only 30 actually do, the model is "overconfident" — miscalibrated.

🎓 中階版本

把預測機率切成 10 個 bin，每 bin 內 (預測機率 vs 實際正例比) 應該落在 45° 線上。差距用 ECE = Σ |freq − predicted| 量化；理想值 ≤ 0.05。臨床 LR 通常校準較好；DL 普遍過度自信。

Split predicted probabilities into 10 bins; each bin's (mean predicted, observed positive rate) should sit on the 45° line. Gap quantified by ECE = Σ|freq − pred|; ≤ 0.05 is "good." Clinical LR is usually well-calibrated; DL is generally overconfident.

🔬 進階版本

Brier 分解：BS = Reliability − Resolution + Uncertainty。Reliability 越小越好（校準）、Resolution 越大越好（能區分）、Uncertainty 是基準率本身的變異。Hosmer-Lemeshow 卡方檢定可正式檢驗，但對大樣本過敏感；改用 Spiegelhalter z-test 或 Calibration-in-the-large + slope。

Brier decomposition: BS = Reliability − Resolution + Uncertainty. Lower reliability = better calibration; higher resolution = better separability; uncertainty is the base-rate variance. Hosmer-Lemeshow χ² is the classic test but oversensitive in big samples; prefer Spiegelhalter z-test or Calibration-in-the-large + slope.

校準

校準錯了會怎樣？

校準錯誤對臨床致命：若模型說「死亡風險 30%」但真實只有 5%，醫師基於這個過高估計可能採取激進治療，反而傷害病人。常見修正方法：

  • Platt scaling：logit 的 logistic 回歸校準。適合二分類、小資料 (n < 1000)、單調平移。
  • Isotonic regression：非參數，但需較多資料 (n > 1000)；能處理非單調。
  • Temperature scaling：DL 最常用，單一參數 T；保持 AUROC 不變。
  • Beta calibration：對機率邊緣端更穩（避免 Platt 在 0/1 端的飽和問題）。
  • Histogram binning：最簡單但精度差，僅做 baseline。

Miscalibration is clinically dangerous: if a model says "30% mortality risk" but the truth is 5%, doctors may overtreat and harm patients. Common fixes:

  • Platt scaling: logistic regression on logits. Best for binary, small data (n < 1000), monotonic shift.
  • Isotonic regression: non-parametric but data-hungry (n > 1000); handles non-monotonic.
  • Temperature scaling: most common for DL — single parameter T; preserves AUROC.
  • Beta calibration: more robust at probability extremes (avoids Platt's saturation near 0/1).
  • Histogram binning: simplest but coarse; use as baseline only.

Brier 分解 + ECE / MCE

為什麼 Brier 比 logloss 更好讀？

Brier score 的定義很簡單：BS = (1/N) Σ (pi − yi)²，就是均方誤差。但它可以分解（Murphy 1973）：

BS = Reliability − Resolution + Uncertainty
  • Reliability：每個 bin 內 (預測 − 觀察) 的加權平方平均；越低越好。
  • Resolution：每個 bin 預測值與全體基準率的差異；越高越好（區分得開）。
  • Uncertainty：基準率 p̄(1−p̄)；資料本身的「天花板難度」。

校準指標族：

ECE = Σm (|Bm|/n) · |acc(Bm) − conf(Bm)|
MCE = maxm |acc(Bm) − conf(Bm)|

ECE 是各 bin 平均，MCE 是最壞 bin。臨床通常 ECE < 0.05 + MCE < 0.10 視為可接受（Naeini 2015 AAAI）。注意：DL 模型未做 Temperature scaling 前，ECE 常落在 0.1–0.3，根本不夠用。

Platt vs Isotonic 怎麼選：Niculescu-Mizil & Caruana (2005 ICML) 經典結論——若 reliability curve 接近 sigmoid 形（boosting / NN 典型）→ Platt；若是任意非單調形（RF / SVM）→ Isotonic；資料 < 1000 一律用 Platt 避免過擬合。

Brier is just MSE: BS = (1/N) Σ (pi − yi)². But Murphy (1973) decomposed it:

BS = Reliability − Resolution + Uncertainty
  • Reliability: weighted MSE of (predicted − observed) per bin; lower is better.
  • Resolution: distance of bin predictions from overall base rate; higher is better (separability).
  • Uncertainty: p̄(1−p̄) — the "data-intrinsic ceiling."

Calibration metric family:

ECE = Σm (|Bm|/n) · |acc(Bm) − conf(Bm)|
MCE = maxm |acc(Bm) − conf(Bm)|

ECE is bin-mean; MCE is worst-bin. Clinical practice typically accepts ECE < 0.05 and MCE < 0.10 (Naeini 2015 AAAI). Note: untuned DL models routinely sit at ECE 0.1–0.3 — far from clinical-grade.

Platt vs Isotonic: Niculescu-Mizil & Caruana (ICML 2005) — if the reliability curve is sigmoid-shaped (typical for boosting / NN) → Platt; if arbitrary non-monotonic (RF / SVM) → Isotonic; data < 1000 → always Platt to avoid overfit.

案例故事

兩個真實故事

2017–2021 · Epic Sepsis Model 撤回時間軸

Epic Sepsis Model 的撤回時間軸

2017：Epic（全美最大 EHR 廠商）推出敗血症預測模型「ESM」，內部驗證 AUC 0.76。短時間內被約 180 家醫院採用，整合進 EHR 預警。

2021-07：Wong et al. (JAMA Internal Medicine) 在密西根大學 38,455 個住院病人上做外部驗證——AUC 掉到 0.63、PPV 僅 7%、敏感度 33%。模型對 67% 真實敗血症完全沒有警示，反過來每 100 次警示有 93 次是假警報。

2022-2023：Epic 緊急撤回原模型，改推「Sepsis 2.0」並啟動再訓練。多家醫院因 alert fatigue 暫停使用。FDA 後續以此案為 PCCP 的主要動機。

檢討要點：(1) 訓練資料 = 開發中心 single-site；(2) 從未報告 calibration 或 decision curve；(3) 沒有跨機構外部驗證；(4) 標籤定義（敗血症 ICD code）跨機構分歧大。每一項都是 TRIPOD-AI 2024 明確要求的。

2017: Epic (US's largest EHR vendor) ships its sepsis prediction model "ESM" at internal AUC 0.76. Within months, ~180 hospitals integrate it as an EHR alert.

July 2021: Wong et al. (JAMA Internal Medicine) externally validates on 38,455 U Michigan inpatients — AUC drops to 0.63, PPV is just 7%, sensitivity 33%. The model misses 67% of true sepsis; 93/100 alerts are false.

2022-2023: Epic withdraws the original model, ships "Sepsis 2.0" and retrains. Several hospitals pause use due to alert fatigue. FDA later cites this case as the primary motivation for PCCP.

Forensics: (1) training data = single development site; (2) calibration / decision curve never reported; (3) no cross-institution external validation; (4) label definition (sepsis ICD code) varies wildly across sites. Each one is now explicitly required by TRIPOD-AI 2024.

教訓：上線前不報 calibration + 跨站外部驗證的 AI = 把醫師當白老鼠。
Lesson: shipping AI without calibration + multi-site external validation = treating clinicians as test subjects.
2022 · DeepMind 心衰預測 calibration 修正

心衰模型如何靠校準翻盤

2022 年 Google DeepMind 與 NHS Royal Free Hospital 合作開發心衰 30 天再住院預測模型。第一版 AUROC 0.84 看起來漂亮，但 reliability diagram 顯示嚴重過度自信——預測 80% 的病人實際只有 50% 再住院。

團隊沒有重訓整個模型，只在驗證集上做 Temperature scaling（Guo et al. 2017 ICML 的單參數 T）。T = 1.7 後，ECE 從 0.18 降到 0.04，臨床 DCA 在 0.1–0.4 threshold 區間 net benefit 翻倍。AUROC 保持 0.84 不變（這是 temperature scaling 的特性）。

關鍵：他們在驗證集而非測試集上 fit T，並在外部 site 重做檢核——避免 calibration 本身過擬合。

In 2022, Google DeepMind partnered with NHS Royal Free Hospital on a 30-day heart-failure readmission model. V1 hit AUROC 0.84 — looked great. But the reliability diagram showed severe overconfidence: of patients predicted 80% risk, only 50% actually readmitted.

The team didn't retrain the model. They applied Temperature scaling (Guo et al. 2017 ICML, single parameter T) on the validation set. At T = 1.7, ECE dropped 0.18 → 0.04, and net benefit on DCA over thresholds 0.1–0.4 doubled. AUROC stayed at 0.84 (a property of temperature scaling).

Key: they fit T on validation (not test) and re-verified at an external site — preventing calibration itself from overfitting.

教訓：很多看似失敗的模型，其實只是 calibration 沒做。一個 1 參數修正就可能救回臨床價值。
Lesson: many "failed" models just need calibration. A single-parameter fix can rescue clinical value.
迷思 vs 事實

常被誤會的事

① AUROC 0.9 = 上線就好
① AUC 高但 calibration / NB 崩塌的案例極常見
Epic Sepsis、IBM Watson Oncology 都是 AUC 高、上線後失敗的代表。三層級都過才能說「可上線」。 Epic Sepsis and IBM Watson Oncology both shipped at high AUC and failed. Only triple-passing (discrimination + calibration + utility) is "deployable".
② Hosmer-Lemeshow 永遠是正解
② 對大樣本過敏感
N > 10,000 時 H-L p-value 幾乎一定 reject，沒鑑別力。Spiegelhalter z-test、Integrated Calibration Index (ICI)、E:O ratio 是 2024 共識。 At N > 10,000, H-L p-values almost always reject — no discrimination. Spiegelhalter z-test, Integrated Calibration Index (ICI), and E:O ratio are the 2024 consensus replacements.
③ 子族 AUC 都看一下就好
③ TRIPOD-AI 2024 + FDA PCCP 強制要求
Obermeyer 2019（Science）證明：種族中性特徵可能造成下游種族歧視。FDA PCCP 要求 族裔、性別、年齡、保險狀態 子族分析。 Obermeyer 2019 (Science) showed: race-blind features can still cause downstream race bias. FDA PCCP mandates subgroup analysis by race, sex, age, insurance status.
④ Decision Curve 太進階用不到
④ DCA 已是標配
Decision Curve Analysis (Vickers & Elkin 2006) 用 net benefit 把「敏感度 vs 1−特異度」轉成「真正幫到的病人 − 多治療的病人」。BMJ、JAMA、Annals of Internal Medicine 從 2019 起幾乎強制要求。 Decision Curve Analysis (Vickers & Elkin 2006) converts sens/1−spec into "patients truly helped − patients overtreated" via net benefit. BMJ, JAMA, Annals of IM have effectively mandated DCA since 2019.
⑤ 外部驗證只要一個就夠
⑤ 三軸全要
Yang et al. 2024 (Nat Med) 對 17 個影像 AI 跨站驗證——AUC 平均掉 11.2pp。三軸缺一不可：地理 (cross-site)、時間 (temporal validation)、族裔 (cross-ancestry)。 Yang et al. 2024 (Nat Med) cross-site validated 17 imaging AIs — AUC dropped 11.2pp on average. All three axes are required: geographic (cross-site), temporal, ancestral.

import numpy as np
from sklearn.metrics import roc_auc_score, average_precision_score, brier_score_loss
from sklearn.calibration import calibration_curve, CalibratedClassifierCV

# 1. Discrimination
print("AUROC =", roc_auc_score(y, p))
print("AUPRC =", average_precision_score(y, p))

print("Brier =", brier_score_loss(y, p))
prob_true, prob_pred = calibration_curve(y, p, n_bins=10)
# 3. Subgroup AUC

print(mf.by_group)
# 4. Decision Curve (用 dcurves)
from dcurves import dca
df = pd.DataFrame({"y":y, "model":p, "all":1, "none":0})

想得更深

Q1. 為什麼臨床更看 calibration、而 ML 社群偏好 AUROC？ ML benchmark（如 Kaggle）多以 AUROC 排名，因為 threshold-free 比較方便；但臨床醫師需要看「30% 風險」這個具體數字決定是否做侵入性檢查——calibration 才關鍵。 延伸：若你的審稿人只看 AUROC，你會怎麼說服他補做 calibration？
Q1. Why does the clinic care about calibration when ML loves AUROC? ML benchmarks (e.g., Kaggle) rank by AUROC because it's threshold-free; but clinicians need the actual "30% risk" number to decide whether to do an invasive test — calibration is what matters. Extend: how would you convince a reviewer who only looks at AUROC to demand calibration?
Q2. 子族 AUC 是否應該被強制報告？權衡？ 強制報告好處：揭露偏差、保護弱勢。壞處：罕例子族樣本不足會放大隨機誤差、可能造成「樣本太少所以放棄該族」的反向歧視。 參考：FDA PCCP 要求至少報告族裔、性別、年齡；EU AI Act Annex IV 要求 disclose 子族表現差異。
Q2. Should subgroup AUC be mandatory? Trade-offs? Pros: exposes bias, protects minorities. Cons: small-subgroup AUC magnifies noise and can create reverse discrimination ("too few samples — drop the group"). Reference: FDA PCCP mandates race / sex / age subgroups; EU AI Act Annex IV requires disclosure of subgroup performance gaps.
Q3. 外部驗證做到什麼程度才算夠？ 理想是 3+ 機構 × 2+ 時間段 × 2+ 族裔。但中小型實驗室可能只能拿到一家外部醫院的資料——這時要老實寫 limitation；不要把單一外部驗證當作「泛化已證明」。 行動：寫 paper 時把「不能做的驗證」也明確列在 Discussion，反而會贏審稿人尊重。
Q3. How much external validation is "enough"? Ideally 3+ sites × 2+ time periods × 2+ ancestries. But small labs may only access one external hospital — be honest about it. Don't sell a single external validation as "generalization proven." Action: explicitly list what you could NOT validate in the Discussion — reviewers reward honesty.
Q4. AI 的「公平性」與「準確度」是不是必然衝突？ 數學上不一定衝突。Obermeyer 2019 證明：去除種族中性的「醫療成本」代理變數後，模型對黑人病人的準確度反而上升。許多「公平性 vs 準確度 trade-off」是糟糕特徵工程的結果，不是不可改的數學定律。 延伸：你能否舉一個你看過的論文，作者宣稱「為了公平性犧牲了 AUC」其實是 feature engineering 問題？
Q4. Are fairness and accuracy necessarily in conflict? Not always. Obermeyer 2019 showed removing race-blind proxy "medical cost" actually improved accuracy for Black patients. Many "fairness vs accuracy trade-offs" are bad feature engineering, not unavoidable math. Extend: can you name a paper claiming "fairness cost us AUC" that's really a feature problem?
Q5. Decision Curve 的「閾值區間 (threshold range)」怎麼選？ 不能隨意選 0–1。Vickers 建議「臨床合理範圍」——例如 BRCA 篩檢做手術閾值約 10–30%、肺結節活檢閾值約 5–10%。把區間放太寬會稀釋訊號、太窄會看不到 trade-off。 參考：Pencina 2024 BMJ 建議外加 sensitivity analysis 與 confidence band。
Q5. How to pick the threshold range for Decision Curve? Don't blindly do 0–1. Vickers recommends the "clinically reasonable range" — e.g., BRCA-driven surgery 10–30%, lung-nodule biopsy 5–10%. Too wide dilutes signal; too narrow misses trade-offs. Reference: Pencina 2024 BMJ adds sensitivity analysis + confidence bands.
常見問答

6 個常見問題

沒有單一指標。但若硬要選：類平衡且偏 ML 場景 → AUROC；類不平衡 / 罕病 → AUPRC；需要機率解釋（臨床決策） → Brier + Calibration plot；需臨床效益 → DCA Net Benefit。實務上，TRIPOD-AI 要求至少 3 個。 There's no single one. If forced: balanced ML benchmark → AUROC; imbalanced / rare → AUPRC; need probability interpretation → Brier + reliability plot; need clinical utility → DCA net benefit. TRIPOD-AI requires at least three.
主指標必須是 AUPRC（基線 = 盛行率）；輔以 sensitivity at fixed specificity (例 95%) 與 NNS (number needed to screen)。AUROC 在罕病常 0.95+ 看似漂亮，但 PPV 可能 < 5%——這正是 Epic Sepsis 的問題。 Primary metric must be AUPRC (baseline = prevalence); supplement with sensitivity at fixed specificity (e.g., 95%) and NNS. AUROC often looks 0.95+ for rare disease but PPV can be < 5% — Epic Sepsis's exact pitfall.
五步：
① 從訓練集劃出獨立「校準集」（10-20% 樣本）
② 在驗證集畫 reliability diagram + 計算 ECE / MCE
③ 若 ECE > 0.05，依模型類別選 calibrator：DL → Temperature；boosting/NN → Platt；RF/SVM → Isotonic
④ 在 校準集（不是測試集）fit calibrator
⑤ 在獨立測試集驗證 ECE 是否真的下降。避免 calibration 本身過擬合。 Five steps:
① Carve out an independent "calibration set" (10–20% of training)
② On validation, draw reliability + compute ECE / MCE
③ If ECE > 0.05, pick calibrator by model: DL → Temperature; boosting/NN → Platt; RF/SVM → Isotonic
④ Fit calibrator on the calibration set (NOT test)
⑤ Verify ECE actually drops on a held-out test set. Avoid overfitting the calibration step.
三招：
① bootstrap CI：1000 次 bootstrap，報 95% CI；CI 寬到沒有比較意義時，誠實寫 "underpowered"。
② 合併相似族：例如「東亞」「南亞」「東南亞」合併成「亞裔」（但要在 model card 註明）。
③ 主動收集：與 LMIC / 少數族裔醫院合作補資料；FDA PCCP 鼓勵這條路。 Three moves:
① Bootstrap CI: 1000 reps, report 95% CI; when CI is too wide, label "underpowered".
② Pool similar groups: e.g., merge East / South / Southeast Asian into "Asian" (document in model card).
③ Actively collect: partner with LMIC / minority-serving hospitals; FDA PCCP encourages this path.
看研究類型：
• 預後 / 預測模型開發 → TRIPOD-AI 2024（27 項）
• 診斷準確度研究 → STARD-AI 2025（30 項）
• 隨機對照試驗 (RCT) → CONSORT-AI 2020 + SPIRIT-AI 2020
• 系統性回顧 → PRISMA-AI（草案中）
三個都可同時用，互補不衝突。 By study type:
• Prognostic / prediction model dev → TRIPOD-AI 2024 (27 items)
• Diagnostic accuracy → STARD-AI 2025 (30 items)
• RCT → CONSORT-AI 2020 + SPIRIT-AI 2020
• Systematic review → PRISMA-AI (draft)
All three can co-apply — complementary, not conflicting.
ROC 答「能不能分」；DCA 答「分了之後值不值得」。
• ROC 只看 TP / FP rate，沒有「治療成本」概念。
• DCA 加入「治療一個 false positive 的代價 ≈ 漏一個 true positive 的代價 × (1−pt)/pt」。
例：BRCA 帶因者預測，pt = 0.2 表示「我願意做 4 次預防性手術換 1 次真陽性」；高 pt 區間（保守）模型可能無 net benefit。 ROC asks "can we separate?"; DCA asks "is separating worth it?"
• ROC sees only TP / FP rate, no concept of treatment cost.
• DCA encodes "cost of one FP treatment ≈ cost of missing one TP × (1−pt)/pt".
Example: BRCA carrier prediction at pt = 0.2 means "I'll do 4 prophylactic surgeries for one true positive"; at high pt (conservative), the model may have zero net benefit.
  1. 三層級評估缺一不可：區分 (AUROC/AUPRC) + 校準 (Brier/ECE) + 臨床效益 (DCA)。Epic Sepsis 撤回的根本原因就是只報區分能力。
  2. AUPRC 是罕病的主指標，不是 AUROC。基線 = 盛行率，比 AUROC 更敏感反映「lift」。
  3. Calibration 可救回模型。DeepMind 心衰預測靠 1 參數 Temperature Scaling 把 ECE 從 0.18 砍到 0.04。
  4. TRIPOD-AI 2024 是 BMJ 共識：27 項清單，子族 + 外部驗證 + 模型卡是硬性要求；不是「nice-to-have」。
  5. 外部驗證要跨三軸：機構、時間、族裔。單一外部 site 不算泛化證明。Yang 2024：跨站 AUC 平均掉 11.2pp。
  6. FDA PCCP + EU AI Act 已生效，子族公平性 + audit trail 不只是學術要求——是法規要求。
  1. All three eval layers are mandatory: discrimination (AUROC/AUPRC) + calibration (Brier/ECE) + clinical utility (DCA). Epic Sepsis was withdrawn for reporting only discrimination.
  2. AUPRC, not AUROC, is the primary metric for rare disease. Baseline = prevalence; reflects "lift" better.
  3. Calibration can rescue models. DeepMind's HF predictor went from ECE 0.18 → 0.04 with a single-parameter Temperature Scaling.
  4. TRIPOD-AI 2024 (BMJ consensus): a 27-item checklist; subgroups + external validation + model card are required, not nice-to-have.
  5. External validation must span three axes: site, time, ancestry. A single external dataset is not proof of generalization. Yang 2024: AUC drops 11.2pp on average.
  6. FDA PCCP + EU AI Act are now in force. Subgroup fairness + audit trails aren't academic niceties — they're regulatory mandates.
