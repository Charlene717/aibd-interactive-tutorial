基礎

生存分析特殊在哪？

普通迴歸假設你能觀察到所有結果。生存分析必須處理「審查 (censoring)」——某些病人在追蹤結束前還沒發生事件（死亡 / 復發 / 失敗），他們的真實時間是「至少 T」但未知。這個結構讓我們不能直接用 MSE 或交叉熵。

核心物件是 hazard function h(t|x) = P(T = t | T ≥ t, x)，以及 partial likelihood (Cox)。當 hazard 對 covariates 為線性時，是標準 CoxPH；用 DNN 取代線性層即 DeepSurv。

Ordinary regression assumes all outcomes are observed. Survival analysis must handle censoring: some patients haven't had the event by the end of follow-up; their true time is "≥ T". This structure forbids plain MSE / cross-entropy.

The core object is the hazard h(t|x) = P(T = t | T ≥ t, x) and the partial likelihood (Cox). When hazard is linear in covariates → standard CoxPH; replace the linear layer with a DNN → DeepSurv.

評估指標

五個必看指標

① C-index (Concordance)

把預測風險與實際存活時間配對是否一致；0.5 隨機、1.0 完美。最常用、但對重度審查不敏感。

Pairwise concordance between predicted risk and actual time; 0.5 random, 1.0 perfect. Common but insensitive to heavy censoring.

② Time-dependent AUC

固定 t* 的 ROC AUC，可看不同時間點區分能力。

ROC AUC at fixed t*, shows discrimination across horizons.

③ Brier Score / IBS

機率預測的均方誤差；IBS 是時間積分版本。低越好。

MSE of probability predictions; IBS integrates over time. Lower is better.

④ IPCW Adjustment

Inverse Probability of Censoring Weighting：校正審查機率對指標的偏差。

Inverse Probability of Censoring Weighting corrects for censoring bias.

⑤ Calibration Plot

觀察「預測 5 年存活」與「實際 5 年存活」的對齊。Hosmer-Lemeshow-like 檢定可量化。

Compare predicted vs observed 5-year survival; Hosmer-Lemeshow-style tests quantify miscalibration.

⑥ Decision Curve Analysis

不同 threshold 下的「淨效益 (net benefit)」。臨床導入前必看。

Net benefit across thresholds. A must-check before deployment.

import torch, torchtuples as tt
from pycox.models import CoxPH
from pycox.evaluation import EvalSurv
net = tt.practical.MLPVanilla(in_features=512, num_nodes=[128,64],
                                  out_features=1, batch_norm=True, dropout=.3)
m = CoxPH(net, tt.optim.Adam(1e-3))
log = m.fit(x_tr, (dur_tr, evt_tr), batch_size=256, epochs=100,
            val_data=(x_va,(dur_va,evt_va)))
m.compute_baseline_hazards()
surv = m.predict_surv_df(x_te)

print("C-index:", ev.concordance_td(),

想再深一點？Cox partial likelihood

Cox PH 模型避開「baseline hazard」的具體假設，用partial likelihood：

L(β) = ∏_{i: δᵢ=1} [ exp(β^T xᵢ) / Σ_{j∈R(tᵢ)} exp(β^T xⱼ) ]

其中 R(tᵢ) 是時間 tᵢ 仍 at risk 的個案集合，δᵢ=1 表示事件發生（未審查）。關鍵假設：(1) proportional hazards—hazard ratio 不隨時間變；(2) independent censoring—審查機率與真實生存時間獨立。

DeepSurv 改裝：把 β^T x 換成 DNN 輸出 f(x)，loss 變成：

L(θ) = − Σ_{i: δᵢ=1} [ f_θ(xᵢ) − log Σ_{j∈R(tᵢ)} exp(f_θ(xⱼ)) ]

仍保留比例假設，但允許非線性 covariate 效應。

DeepHit 跳脫比例假設：直接離散時間軸成 K bins，輸出每個 bin 的 cause-specific hazard；可建模競爭風險（cancer death vs cardiovascular death）。

Cox PH avoids assuming a specific baseline hazard, using partial likelihood:

L(β) = ∏_{i: δᵢ=1} [ exp(β^T xᵢ) / Σ_{j∈R(tᵢ)} exp(β^T xⱼ) ]

where R(tᵢ) is the at-risk set at time tᵢ, δᵢ=1 means event observed (not censored). Key assumptions: (1) proportional hazards — hazard ratios don't change over time; (2) independent censoring — censoring probability independent of true survival.

DeepSurv twist: replace β^T x with DNN output f(x); loss becomes:

L(θ) = − Σ_{i: δᵢ=1} [ f_θ(xᵢ) − log Σ_{j∈R(tᵢ)} exp(f_θ(xⱼ)) ]

Still proportional, but allows non-linear covariate effects.

DeepHit drops proportionality: discretizes time into K bins, outputs cause-specific hazards per bin; models competing risks (cancer death vs cardiovascular death).

案例故事
2024 · TCGA-BRCA DeepSurv 在台灣驗證

DeepSurv 在台灣醫院 collapse

2023 一個 TCGA-BRCA cohort 上訓練的乳癌 DeepSurv 模型，C-index 0.78，發在 JAMA Network Open。台北某醫學中心 2024 想驗證能否導入臨床——對 1500 名本地乳癌患者外部驗證。

結果：C-index 0.61。原因深挖：(1) TCGA 多為晚期患者，台灣有規律乳房 X 光篩檢使早期診斷比例高 ~30%；(2) ER+ 比例差（TCGA 75% vs 台灣 80% 多）；(3) 治療 protocol 差（TCGA 多 anthracycline-based，台灣較多 taxane）。

更糟：calibration 崩潰——high-risk 預測過於悲觀（預測 5 年死亡 40% 但實際 18%）。若按此模型把 high-risk 病人推進激進化療，會傷害過多病人。

團隊改 retrain on 台灣本地 cohort（N=8000），C-index 提升到 0.74，calibration slope 從 0.51 → 0.92。外部驗證 + 本地 retrain 是 AI 臨床導入的鐵律。

In 2023 a TCGA-BRCA-trained breast cancer DeepSurv model achieved C-index 0.78, published in JAMA Network Open. A Taipei medical center wanted to validate clinical deployment in 2024 — external validation on 1,500 local breast cancer patients.

Result: C-index 0.61. Root causes: (1) TCGA skews to late-stage; Taiwan's regular mammography screening yields ~30% more early diagnoses; (2) ER+ proportions differ (TCGA 75% vs Taiwan ~80%+); (3) treatment protocols differ (TCGA anthracycline-based, Taiwan more taxane).

Worse: calibration collapse — high-risk predictions over-pessimistic (40% predicted 5-year mortality vs 18% actual). Pushing "high-risk" patients into aggressive chemo would harm many.

The team retrained on local Taiwan cohort (N=8,000), C-index improved to 0.74, calibration slope 0.51 → 0.92. External validation + local retraining is an iron law of clinical AI deployment.

教訓：(1) 訓練資料偏差會傳到臨床；(2) calibration 比 C-index 更影響臨床決策；(3) 本地 retrain 不是 luxury，是必須。
Lessons: (1) training bias propagates to clinic; (2) calibration affects decisions more than C-index; (3) local retraining isn't a luxury — it's a must.

想得更深

Q1. 什麼時候 DeepSurv 真的勝過 Cox？2024 食道癌研究：DeepSurv 0.74 vs Cox 0.72 (+2pp)。所以「勝過」常常微弱。當 covariate 有強非線性（影像、基因組）或交互作用時，DL 才顯著贏。建議：高維/影像/序列輸入用 DL；tabular 結構化資料 Cox 已夠。
Q1. When does DeepSurv really beat Cox?2024 esophageal study: DeepSurv 0.74 vs Cox 0.72 (+2pp). The "win" is often marginal. DL gains meaningfully only with strong non-linearity (imaging, genomics) or interactions.Rule of thumb: high-dim / imaging / sequence → DL; tabular structured → Cox suffices.
Q2. 競爭風險為什麼比想像中重要？老年 cohort 心衰研究若忽略 CVD death，會嚴重 over-estimate cancer-specific survival。Fine-Gray 是傳統解法；DeepHit 在 deep learning 端。參考：Wolbers 2014 對 competing risks 的臨床易誤用警示。
Q2. Why are competing risks more important than expected?Elderly cohort heart-failure studies ignoring CVD death severely over-estimate cancer-specific survival. Fine-Gray is the classical fix; DeepHit on the DL side.Reference: Wolbers 2014 warns of common clinical misuse of competing risks.
Q3. 把影像 + 臨床合併會更好嗎？WSI MIL embedding + 臨床特徵的 hybrid DeepSurv 通常 C-index +3-5pp。但 over-fitting 風險高，需要嚴格 leave-one-center-out validation。關鍵：影像來源跨機構越多，混合 model 越穩。
Q3. Does combining imaging + clinical features really help?WSI MIL embedding + clinical-feature hybrid DeepSurv usually +3-5pp C-index. But overfitting risk is high; requires strict leave-one-center-out validation.Key: the more institutions for imaging, the more robust the hybrid model.
Q4. 為何 Decision Curve 比 C-index 更重要？C-index 高但 calibration 差的模型，臨床上「永遠都會推進治療」或「永遠都會省略」。Decision Curve 直接顯示「在各種 threshold 下淨效益」，是 TRIPOD-AI 2024 強制要報的。行動：每個生存模型都要報 Decision Curve at 1y, 3y, 5y。
Q4. Why is Decision Curve more important than C-index?A high-C-index but poorly-calibrated model "always treats" or "always skips". Decision Curve directly shows net benefit per threshold; TRIPOD-AI 2024 mandates it.Action: every survival model should report Decision Curve at 1y, 3y, 5y.
